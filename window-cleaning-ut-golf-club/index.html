What UT Golf Club homes are like

The homes here sit around the University of Texas Golf Club, and most were built to look out at it. That means big picture windows, tall glass at the back, and plenty of sliding doors onto patios and covered porches. Streets like Fairway Cove, Old Course Drive and University Club Drive wind through the section, and it is the biggest gated pocket in Steiner Ranch, with hundreds of homes. Prices run roughly $900K to $3M, so you see everything from solid family homes to large custom builds. A lot of them are two story with a lot of glass facing the course and the Hill Country beyond it.

Because it is gated and run by its own sub association, access is a real part of every visit. Owners usually get us on the list ahead of time so the gate is simple.

Streets and landmarks: Fairway Cove, Old Course Drive, University Club Drive.

What fairway glass deals with

Golf course irrigation is the first thing we notice. Overspray from sprinkler heads lands on glass and dries into white mineral spots, and over a few seasons that can etch into the surface if nobody removes it. We treat those with hard water spot removal at $10 per pane when ordinary cleaning will not lift them. The course also means open sky and long sight lines, so afternoon sun on west and south facing glass shows every streak and film. Add cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April, and the glass picks up a yellow film fast.

Dust from the course and nearby trees gathers in screens and tracks, so we often clean both while we are there.

How we work UT Golf Club

We run a dual RO, four stage pure water system with a DI resin tank, so the water is close to 0 TDS and glass dries clear with no soap film. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, which keeps ladders off your siding, stone and landscaping. Inside, we clean by hand with squeegees, lay drop cloths, and use ladders with cotton covers on two story rooms. Gated sections take a little planning, so tell us the gate code or the guest list name when you book. We group UT Golf Club visits with nearby Steiner Ranch homes when we can, which keeps scheduling tight, usually within two weeks.

A 3,500 sq ft home here is about $350 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft. Plan on roughly two and a half to three and a half hours outside, and about an hour and three quarters if you add the inside.

UT Golf Club questions

Do you need a gate code to clean my UT Golf Club home?

Yes, we will ask for the code or have your name on the guest list before the visit. Text it to us when you book, and we will arrive within the window we agreed on so you are not waiting at the gate.

Can you remove the white spots from course sprinklers?

Usually. Light spotting comes off during a normal pure water clean. Heavier or older mineral spots on glass get hard water spot removal at $10 per pane, and we will tell you before we start if any glass looks etched.

Do you clean golf view windows on a second or third story?

Yes. Our water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so exterior glass is done without ladders against the house. Interior glass on tall rooms is done by hand with cotton covered ladders and drop cloths.

How much does a typical UT Golf Club home cost?

Outside glass runs $10 per window, or about $0.10 per sq ft of house. Figure about $350 for a 3,500 sq ft home. Adding the inside brings UT Golf Club homes to $15 per window. No job runs under $200, and the quote comes to your phone by text.
