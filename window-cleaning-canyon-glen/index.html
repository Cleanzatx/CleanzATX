What Canyon Glen homes are like

Canyon Glen is a quiet corner. The homes sit on a circle that ends in a cul de sac, with lots that back toward the canyon. Prices run roughly $800K to $1.3M. It is a small section, not gated, and under the Steiner Ranch Master Association. Cul de sac streets are quiet, with little through traffic, and the lot lines usually give a bit more rear space than on a standard block. The homes tend to open to the back, where the canyon view is, with large windows and sliding doors on the rear elevation.

Because the street is short and compact, a visit here is quick to organize.

Streets and landmarks: Canyon Glen Circle.

What Canyon Glen windows deal with

Canyon backs mean native trees and brush close to the house. That brings pollen, spider webs, leaf litter and dust. Cedar pollen in winter and live oak pollen in spring coat glass in a yellow film, and tracks and screens collect twigs and webs. Wind coming up the canyon carries dust onto the rear windows. Sprinklers along the back wall also leave hard water spots on the lower panes, and we treat those at $10 per pane if regular cleaning cannot lift them. Glass facing the canyon gets sun at different times of day depending on the lot, so we check each side in angled light.

Clean windows are the whole point of a view like this.

How we work Canyon Glen

Cul de sacs ask for some courtesy. We park along the curb out of the way, avoid blocking driveways, and keep the work quiet. Rear windows are the main job, so we ask that side gates are unlocked and pets are inside. Our pure water system, a dual RO unit with a DI resin tank, delivers near 0 TDS water, which dries with no spots. Poles reach up to three stories, so no ladders touch the rear stone or siding, and the slope behind the house stays out of it. Canyon Glen is a natural pairing with Bella Mar and Palisades, and we generally schedule within two weeks.

A 3,500 sq ft home is about $350 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft. Plan about two and a half to three and a half hours outside, plus roughly an hour and three quarters inside.

Canyon Glen questions

Can you clean windows on a sloped lot behind the house?

Yes. Water fed poles work from the ground and reach up to three stories, so we do not need to set a ladder on uneven or sloping ground behind the home.

Do you park in the cul de sac?

We park out of the way along the curb and do not block driveways or mailboxes. If there is a spot you prefer, text us and we will use it.

Why do the rear windows get dirtier than the front?

Canyon and brush backs bring more pollen, webs and dust, and wind funnels dirt onto the rear glass. Sprinklers along the back wall add mineral spotting as well.

How much is a normal Canyon Glen job?

Glass outside is $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft, which puts a house of 3,500 sq ft near $350. Inside and out is $15 per window, with a $200 minimum job.

Can I get regular cleanings?

Yes. The Clean Windows Club offers 10% off twice a year, 15% off Triannual and Quarterly, and 20% off Every 2 Months, with the 7 day rain guarantee on the upper tiers.
