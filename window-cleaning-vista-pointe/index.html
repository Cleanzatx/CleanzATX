What Vista Pointe homes are like

If Lake Pointe has a view section, this is it. Vista Pointe sits up where the land opens toward the Hill Country and Lake Austin, and its homes range from about $700,000 to $1.2 million. The Lake Pointe HOA covers the section, and there is no gate. Houses are designed to face outward, with tall back windows, wide sliders and decks or patios that take in the view.

That design makes glass the main feature of the house, so how it looks matters more here than in a house with small windows.

What Vista Pointe windows deal with

Big windows facing out catch everything. Westward panes get hit by afternoon sun, and any streak or film shows plainly. Wind on higher ground carries pollen, dust and Highway 71 haze onto the glass, and sprinkler spray adds mineral spots on the lower sections. In winter, cedar pollen turns a clear view yellow. By March, live oak adds its own coating.

Pure water makes the difference with view glass. The dual RO, 4 stage system with a DI resin tank gives near 0 TDS water, so the panes dry clear with no soap film to catch the light. Stone and stucco are soft washed, never pressure washed.

How we work Vista Pointe

We clean view glass last in the visit, after frames and walls, so nothing drips onto finished panes. For tall back windows we use the poles from the ground, and if a deck gets in the way, we work from the deck without moving furniture further than needed. Many owners schedule early in the day so the sun is off the back glass while we work. Screens are worth cleaning here too, since dirty mesh blurs the view as much as dirty glass.

A 3,200 square foot example: exterior windows at about $320 and roughly two and a half to three hours on site. Interior added at $15 a window would bring the total close to $480.

Vista Pointe questions

Why do my view windows show streaks at sunset?

Soap film and mineral residue catch low light. Pure water leaves nothing behind, so streaks are far less likely.

Do you clean windows on decks and balconies?

Yes. We reach glass from the deck or from the ground with poles, and we handle railings glass if it is part of the job.

Do screens really affect the view?

Dirty screens dull light and blur outlines. Cleaning screens costs $5 each and often surprises owners with how much brighter rooms become.

How often does view glass need washing?

Many view homes do well with Triannual or Quarterly service. Both take 15% off and include a free yearly screen cleaning.

What does a quote look like?
