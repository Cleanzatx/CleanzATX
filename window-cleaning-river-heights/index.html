What River Heights homes are like

River Heights is where many people start in Steiner Ranch. The homes date from the 1990s, run about 1,800 to 2,500 sq ft, and sell roughly between $450K and $650K. There are hundreds of them, and the section is not gated, with the Steiner Ranch Master Association over it. Houses are mostly one and two story, with a decent amount of glass in the front rooms and a few sliding doors out back. Mature trees have had thirty years to grow, which gives the streets shade and character.

These are family homes that get used, and the windows show it.

What River Heights windows deal with

Older homes mean older sprinkler habits. Thirty years of spray on lower windows can leave stubborn white mineral spots and sometimes a hazy etch. We test a pane first, and if it will not come clean with pure water we offer hard water spot removal at $10 per pane. Mature trees add pollen from cedar in the winter and live oak in spring, along with leaf litter and spiders in the screens and tracks. Screens often have never been cleaned, so a quick screen wash at $5 each, or $10 for full screens, can change how the room looks.

Older aluminum or vinyl frames can hold dust, so we wipe those as we go.

How we work River Heights

This is a simple route. No gates, close driveways, and homes small enough to do in a morning. We use a dual RO, four stage pure water system, so there is no soap film and nothing to wipe off afterward. For two story homes our water fed poles work from the ground, so no ladders go against your siding. Interior windows are cleaned by hand. Many River Heights owners start with exterior glass only, which is the best value, and add inside later. We pair River Heights with other Steiner Ranch visits and book within two weeks as a rule.

For a 2,000 sq ft home, exterior windows come to about $200 at $0.10 per sq ft, which is also the $200 minimum job. Plan on about an hour and a half to two hours outside, and an hour for the interior.

River Heights questions

Is it worth cleaning windows that have never been done well?

Yes. Dirt and mineral spots that have built up for years usually come off or improve a lot with pure water. If a pane is etched, we tell you before we start so there are no surprises.

What is the cheapest way to start?

Exterior windows only. At $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft, a 2,000 sq ft home is about $200, which is also our minimum job. You can add inside windows and screens later.

Do you clean screens and tracks too?

Yes. Standard screens are $5, full screens $10, solar screens $15, and tracks $5. Older screens that have never been washed often get a dramatic improvement.

Can you do a two story home with no ladder?

Exterior glass, yes. Our water fed poles can work up to three stories while we stand on the ground. Inside two story rooms we use ladders with cotton covers and drop cloths.

Do you offer a repeat discount?

Yes. The Clean Windows Club gives 10% off twice a year, 15% off Triannual or Quarterly, and 20% off Every 2 Months, with a 7 day rain guarantee on the upper tiers.
