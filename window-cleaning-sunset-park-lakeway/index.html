What Sunset Park windows deal with

West facing glass takes strong afternoon light in a neighborhood like this one. Any smear stands out when the sun drops low. Pollen settles heavily on those panes, cedar from December to February and live oak from March to April.

Twenty years of sprinklers leave a visible mineral layer on lower windows. On 2000s homes we often see old hard water stains that regular washing will not lift. Spot removal costs $10 per pane. Tracks collect webs and leaf bits, and screens loosen with age.
