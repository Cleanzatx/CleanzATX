Pricing questions

How much does window cleaning cost in Lakeway and Bee Cave?

Exterior window cleaning is $10 per window (or $5 per pane or $0.10 per sq ft, whichever is lower). Adding interior glass is $5 per window. The minimum job is $150, and a typical home runs about $350.

Do you charge by the window, the pane, or the square foot?

We use whichever comes out lower for your home: $10 per window, $5 per pane, or $0.10 per square foot for exterior glass. Your quote shows the method we used.

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes. The minimum job is $150.

Do you offer any discounts?

Military and seniors get 10% off. Clean Windows Club members save 5% to 20% on every visit. Our fall promotion takes 10% off a first window cleaning with code FALL, screen cleaning is currently free with any window cleaning, and referring a neighbor earns a $50 account credit when they book.
