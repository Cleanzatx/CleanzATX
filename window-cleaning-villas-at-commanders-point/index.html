What Villas at Commanders Point homes are like

Roughly 25 villas make up this community, set just west of Mansfield Dam along Mansfield Dam Court. Homes run about 3,600 sq ft and sell for around $760K when one comes up, which does not happen often. A gate controls entry, and the Villas at Commanders Point HOA runs the shared grounds.

Homes this size have a good amount of glass, with big living area windows, upstairs bedrooms and entry panels.

Mansfield Dam Court

What Villas at Commanders Point windows deal with

The dam and lake are close, so mist and hard water travel on the wind and settle on glass. When it dries, it leaves a haze of white spots, strongest on the south and west sides where sun bakes it on. Sprinkler overspray along landscaped beds does the same thing to lower panes.

The community is also near busy roads and a marina, so dust and road grime add to it. Cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April cover the screens. We clean screens at $5 each and treat mineral spots at $10 per pane.

How we work Villas at Commanders Point

The gate means we need a code or a contact name before the day. Space inside a small community can be tight, so we park where the HOA allows and run the pure water system from the truck. Water fed poles let us reach upper windows up to three stories from the ground, so there are no ladders against stucco or planters. Villas this size work well with a house soft wash in the same trip.

At $0.10 per sq ft, a 3,600 sq ft villa comes to about $360 for the outside of the windows. Expect around three to three and a half hours on site, a bit less if the weather is dry.
