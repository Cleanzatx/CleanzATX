The Steiner Ranch neighborhoods

Steiner Ranch is one master planned community, but it is big enough to work like a town of its own, and every section has its own page in the grid below. The UT Golf Club is the largest gated section, hundreds of homes around the University of Texas course on Fairway Cv, Old Course Dr and University Club Dr. Bella Mar is the newer section with its own amenity center and pools on canyon and greenbelt lots. Bellagio Estates, 30 estate sites on Flat Top Ranch Rd, and Lakewood Hills on Lakewood Hills Ter are the private gated enclaves with the largest custom homes. Sierra Vista holds the one acre plus homesites on the high ground.

Closer to the water, River Dance runs along Tierra Grande Trl near River Ridge Elementary with premium pricing for its Lake Austin views, and River Heights and Parkside are the original 1990s sections with the lowest price of entry. Caslano and Palisades are the newer streets near the shops, Canyon Glen is the cul de sac pocket on Canyon Glen Cir, and Longhorn Village is the retirement campus on Longhorn Pkwy. Past the end of S Quinlan Park Rd are Lakeland Park, River Bend and Hidden Valley, the older Lake Austin waterfront pockets with lots up to an acre and a half. All of it sits in 78732 and Leander ISD.

Streets and landmarks: Steiner Ranch Blvd, Quinlan Park Rd, RM 620, University Club Dr, Flat Top Ranch Rd.

What Steiner Ranch windows deal with

Steiner Ranch was built in phases from the 1990s into the 2010s, so most homes are two story stucco and limestone with big rear glass looking over a canyon, the golf course or Lake Austin. Irrigation is the first problem we look for. Nearly every lot has a sprinkler system, the UT Golf Club fairways add their own overspray, and the water here is hard enough to leave white rings on the lower panes within a season. Left alone through a few summers those rings etch into the glass. We treat them at $10 per pane, and on plans at Triannual and above, five windows a visit are included in the price.

The canyons that make the views also feed the dust. Cedar covers the slopes down to the lake and throws pollen from December through February, with live oak following in March and April, and the wind off the water pushes both onto west and south facing glass. Homes near Lake Austin in River Dance, Lakewood Hills and the Quinlan Park Rd pockets add lake spray on the water side. Greenbelt lots in Bella Mar and Canyon Glen collect webs in every screen by midsummer. Pure water is the answer to all of it: our dual RO, four stage system with a DI resin tank dries at near 0 TDS, so the glass stays clear with no film for the next layer to grip.

How we work Steiner Ranch

From our base in Lakeway the route is simple: north on RM 620 across Mansfield Dam, then right on Quinlan Park Rd or Steiner Ranch Blvd, a drive we make every week. Because it is a trip across the dam, we book Steiner Ranch as full route days and group jobs by section, so one truck can work River Dance in the morning and the UT Golf Club after lunch. That density is what keeps the price at $10 per window out here. Gated sections, meaning the UT Golf Club, Bellagio Estates and Lakewood Hills, need a gate code or our name at the call box the day before. The master association has no approval step for exterior window cleaning on a single home.

A typical Steiner Ranch home runs 2,500 to 4,000 sq ft, so exterior window cleaning is about $250 to $400, and a 3,500 sq ft home is about $350. At 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft that is roughly three hours outside; interiors take about half that. Water fed poles reach three stories from the ground, which matters on canyon lots where the rear wall drops two full stories below the main floor. Screens and tracks are $5 each and worth adding on greenbelt lots. Gutter cleaning in the fall and a limestone soft wash, never pressure, round out the usual Steiner Ranch visit. We can usually get you on the schedule within two weeks.

Steiner Ranch questions

Do you really cover Steiner Ranch from Lakeway?

Yes, every week. It is one drive across Mansfield Dam, and we book full days here so the trip is spread across a whole section rather than charged to one house.

How much is window cleaning for a Steiner Ranch home?

Exterior windows are $10 each, which is about $0.10 per sq ft of home, with a $200 minimum. A 3,500 sq ft home is about $350, and inside and out runs $15 per window.

Can you get into the UT Golf Club section?

Yes. Send the gate code or call our name in to the gate the day before. The same goes for Bellagio Estates and Lakewood Hills. We confirm by text the night before.

Why do my lower windows have white rings?

Sprinkler overspray drying on the glass. The water here is hard, and the rings etch if they sit for a few summers. We treat them at $10 per pane, and plans include five a visit.

How often should Steiner Ranch windows be cleaned?

Triannual or Quarterly fits most homes here, timed after cedar season, after oak pollen and before the holidays. Both plans are 15% off and both include the 7 day rain guarantee.

Can you reach the back of a canyon lot home?

Yes. Water fed poles reach three stories from the ground, so the rear wall of a walkout home gets cleaned without any ladder set on the slope or the deck.
