What the villas are like

These are the compact side of Spanish Oaks. Public records describe smaller villas with shared golf access, in a gated enclave managed under the Spanish Oaks POA, with prices around $1.2M to $2.5M. A villa is a different job from an estate: fewer windows, tighter walkways and lots of glass facing a patio or a fairway view.

Many owners here live in Austin or elsewhere part of the year. They want a home that looks cared for when they arrive, without having to be home when the crew comes.

What villa windows deal with

Golf course irrigation overspray is the first thing to watch on glass near a fairway. It lands as white mineral spots, and a home that sits empty for weeks lets those spots bake on in the Texas sun. Pollen from cedar in winter and live oak in spring settles on every pane, and trees near a villa bring webs into the screens.

A closed up house also collects dust on tracks and sills. If your villa has been shut for a month, tracks and screens are worth adding at $5 each so the windows open easily the next time you are in.

How we work the villas

Because the community is gated, we need your gate code or the name of whoever should list us. We can work with a property manager or a neighbor who has a key for interior access, and we send photos when the job is done so you can see it from wherever you are. Smaller homes also pair well with a patio and walkway wash, which we do by hand so the area does not flood.

A 2,000 square foot villa is about $200 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft, which is also our minimum job. Exterior time on site is about 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft, so a visit this size is usually under two hours.

Villas at Spanish Oaks questions

Can you clean while I am out of town?

Yes, for exterior glass. Send the gate code and any access notes. Interior cleaning needs someone to let us in, such as a manager or neighbor. We text photos once the work is finished.

What does a villa usually cost?

Outside glass is $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft. A 2,000 square foot villa is about $200, which is our minimum job. Adding the inside brings it to $15 per window, and screens are $5.

Does fairway overspray leave marks on villa glass?

It can. Irrigation water leaves white mineral spots that dry hard. Pure water cleaning removes the loose film, and stubborn spots are treated at $10 per pane so the glass comes back clear.
