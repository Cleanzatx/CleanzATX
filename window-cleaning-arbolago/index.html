What Arbolago homes are like

The community is small and gated, with estate sized lots and an Arbolago HOA. A private park on Lake Travis gives residents their own piece of shoreline. Home prices run from $1.4M to $2M. The location sits north of the center of Lakeway.

Houses are built to face the water, so the biggest windows tend to look at the lake. Owners here use their outdoor spaces heavily, with terraces, covered patios and pools sitting close to large glass doors. When a home is the owner's main view, clean glass becomes one of the first things guests notice.

What Arbolago windows deal with

Lake exposure brings several issues at once. Spray and humidity leave mineral film. Wind carries pollen and dust across open water onto west facing glass, where afternoon sun shows every smear. Cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen from March to April add to it.

Lawns and planting beds get regular watering, and overspray hits lower panes. Over time those white spots can etch. Our water filtering, dual RO, four stage and a DI resin tank, gets water near 0 TDS, and glass dries with no soap film. For spots already set, hard water removal is $10 per pane.

How we work Arbolago

Gate access comes first. Send us the code or a contact, and we plan arrival around it. We reach the upper and view side glass from the ground with water fed poles, which keeps equipment off terraces and sloped lawn. Inside windows are cleaned by hand with drop cloths down. We also handle house soft wash, stone and stucco, and flatwork around the pool. Limestone is only ever soft washed.

For a 4,500 square foot home, exterior windows come to about $450. Expect roughly three and a half to four and a half hours on site outside. Adding the interior takes about two hours more. Quarterly service takes 15% off.

Arbolago questions

Do you work inside gated communities?

Yes. Send the gate code or the name of someone who can open it. We confirm the arrival window by text before we come.

Can you clean glass above water or steep lawn?

Usually yes. Poles reach up to three stories from the ground, and that keeps ladders off sloped or wet ground. We tell you in advance if something is not safe.

What is hard water etching?

Minerals from lake spray or sprinklers dry on glass and, over time, bite into the surface. Spots caught early come off with removal at $10 per pane. Etched glass may not recover fully.

How much for a large lake home?

About $0.10 per square foot for the exterior, so a 4,500 square foot home is about $450. If you want the inside done too, it is $15 per window for the pair of sides. We quote by text.

Can you do stone and the pool area?

Yes. Stone is soft washed, never pressure washed, and pool decks and patios are cleaned by hand. Soft wash starts at $0.20 per square foot with a $400 minimum.
