Enclave at Commanders Point questions

Do you need to enter my home?

For exterior glass, no. We clean from the ground with water fed poles. For inside windows, someone needs to be home or arrange access, and we cover floors with drop cloths. Text us a day that works and we will confirm the time.

How is the gate handled?

Send the code or a contact when you text. We handle the rest and keep equipment on your side of the property. Your association may have its own vendor rules, so check with your manager if you are unsure.

Can we book more than one townhome at the same time?

Yes. Neighbors who book together help us keep travel short. Text the group details and we can build a visit around it. You do not have to coordinate with a neighbor, but you can if you prefer.

What does it cost for a 2,400 sq ft townhome?
