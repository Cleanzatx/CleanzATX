What The Preserve at Lakeway homes are like

The Preserve holds several hundred homes in a range of types. Sizes run from about 1,959 to 4,200 square feet, and prices go from $400K to $1M. Garden homes sit near larger detached houses, so one visit might be a compact lock and leave home and the next a two story with a wall of glass. The Preserve at Lakeway HOA manages the community, and there is no gate.

The mix means owners have different needs. Some want a quick tidy twice a year, while others want full inside and outside cleaning on a plan.

What The Preserve at Lakeway windows deal with

As the name suggests, there is green space around the homes. Trees and shrubs bring pollen, leaf litter and webs. Cedar pollen peaks from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April. Garden homes with small patios often have glass doors that catch the worst of it.

Shared landscaping is watered on a schedule, and sprinklers hit nearby windows at the same time each week. That repetition builds white mineral spots in the same places. Pure water leaves nothing behind, and hard water removal is $10 per pane for the stubborn ones.

How we work The Preserve at Lakeway

There is no gate, but the HOA may have rules about parking and noise, so mention them when you text. Poles reach upper windows from the ground, which avoids ladders against shared landscaping or fences. We match the work to the home. A garden home is often a quick visit near the minimum, while a bigger house calls for more time and often a soft wash on siding or stucco.

A 3,000 square foot house is about $300 for the exterior. A garden home near 1,959 square feet comes to about $196, which rounds up to our $200 minimum. Bigger homes take roughly 45 to 60 minutes outside per 1,000 square feet.

The Preserve at Lakeway questions

Do you clean smaller garden homes?

Yes. Our minimum job is $200, and garden homes often land at or just above that. Text the address and a rough window count.

Does the HOA allow outside contractors?

Most do. If your HOA asks for proof of insurance, we carry $1M and can send it. Tell us about any parking rules ahead of time.

How often do windows need cleaning here?

Many owners choose Twice a Year, others pick Triannual to match pollen seasons. Triannual takes 15% off and adds a free yearly screen cleaning.

Can you clean glass doors on a small patio?

Yes. We clean glass doors inside and out, and the patio can be washed by hand so water stays under control. Concrete flatwork is $0.18 per square foot.

What if I am not home?

For exterior work you do not need to be. Just make sure we can reach the windows, and tell us about pets or gates.
