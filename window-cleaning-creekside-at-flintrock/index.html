What Creekside at Flintrock East windows deal with

On new glass, the first enemy is construction residue. Stucco dust, paint mist and sticker adhesive dry on the glass and need patient work to come off. After that, sprinkler mineral deposits become the big issue, and white spots on lower panes are common by the second summer. A dual RO, 4 stage pure water system with a DI resin tank gives water near 0 TDS, so the glass dries with no spots and no soap film.

Seasonal pollen matters as well. Cedar pollen runs December to February, and live oak pollen in March and April, and it sticks to damp glass after morning sprinklers. Big back windows facing west show every streak in afternoon light. We clean screens at $5 each and tracks at $5, and hard water removal is $10 per pane.
