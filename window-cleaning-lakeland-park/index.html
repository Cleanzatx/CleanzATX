What Lakeland Park homes are like

Lakeland Park sits at the end of South Quinlan Park Road, close to Lake Austin and well apart from the main Steiner Ranch entrances. It is a small, ungated subdivision with no master HOA listed for it, and it is in Leander ISD. Lots are a quarter acre or more, and prices run about $700K to $1.5M. Larger lots mean the houses are spaced out, often with mature trees, decks and some distance from the road. It feels more like a lake neighborhood than a planned community.

You find a mix of ages and styles, since individual owners built and updated their homes over time.

South Quinlan Park Road

What Lakeland Park windows deal with

Proximity to the lake brings moisture, mineral spray and plenty of wildlife. Mature trees drop leaves and bring webs, so screens and tracks fill up. Cedar pollen in winter and live oak pollen in spring cover the glass in a yellow haze. Sprinkler overspray leaves hard water spots, and with lake humidity those spots seem to cling harder and can etch over time. We treat them with hard water spot removal at $10 per pane when ordinary pure water cleaning does not lift them. West facing glass sees strong afternoon glare, and every streak shows.

Because homes were built at different times, glass varies from older single pane to modern double pane, and we adjust our approach.

How we work Lakeland Park

Lakeland Park is a dead end road, so we plan our parking and keep the work tidy. There is no gate. We bring our own pure water, a dual RO, four stage system with a DI resin tank, and poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we do not need ladders against older siding or fragile trim. Interior glass is hand cleaned with drop cloths. Lakeland Park pairs naturally with River Bend and Hidden Valley, and we try to book neighbors close together, typically within two weeks. A house soft wash is a common add on for lake humidity buildup on siding.

A 4,000 sq ft house on these lots is about $400 for the outside glass. Plan on roughly three to four hours outside and about two hours inside.

Lakeland Park questions

Do you serve homes at the end of Quinlan Park Road?

Yes. Lakeland Park is within our Steiner Ranch area, and we group it with the other Lake Austin pockets nearby. Visits usually land within two weeks, and we text when we are on the way.

Is lake humidity hard on windows?

It can be. Moisture and mineral spray dry onto glass and leave white spots that bond over time. Pure water removes most of it, and we use hard water spot removal at $10 per pane for the rest.

Can you clean the screens under all those trees?

Yes. Plain screens are $5, full screens $10, solar screens $15, and tracks $5. Leaf litter and webs are the usual reason to add them.

What does a Lakeland Park job cost?

Outside glass is billed near $0.10 per sq ft, which makes a 4,000 sq ft Lakeland Park house about $400. Adding the inside brings Lakeland Park homes to $15 per window. The minimum job is $200.

Can you also wash the siding or deck?

Yes. A house soft wash starts at $0.20 per sq ft, $400 minimum. Decks and patios are cleaned by hand so they do not get soaked, starting at $0.18 per sq ft for concrete.
