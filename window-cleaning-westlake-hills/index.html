Window Cleaning Conditions in Westlake Hills

West Lake Hills is a small city just west of downtown Austin along Bee Caves Road. Together with neighboring Rollingwood and the Rob Roy area, it's part of Eanes ISD and is often just called “Westlake.” The terrain is steep and wooded, with homes tucked into hillsides under live oak and cedar.

The housing mix ranges from older ranch-style homes on large wooded lots to newer modern builds with floor-to-ceiling glass and window walls. Big panes and custom frames need careful handling, and our water-fed pole system reaches high windows from the ground without ladders against the glass.

That tree canopy means pollen, leaf litter and spider webs collect on screens, sills and tracks, and shady sides of the house stay damp longer after rain. Add hard-water spots from irrigation, and regular cleaning makes a visible difference on Westlake homes with a lot of glass.
