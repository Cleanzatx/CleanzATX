What Cardinal Hills Estates homes are like

This is a modest sized subdivision of roughly 100 to 200 homes, with a median price around $600K. Some lots sit right on the water and others are only a short walk from it. There is no gate and no known HOA, so each owner handles their own upkeep. It is a different place from the older Cardinal Hills neighborhood nearby, and the two have separate pages on our site.

Homes close to the creek tend to be built to take in the view, which means more glass on the water side than on the street side. Docks, decks and boat gear are part of daily life for many owners here. A clean set of windows ties into the whole waterfront setup.

What Cardinal Hills Estates windows deal with

Water is the main issue. Spray and humidity from the lake side leave mineral deposits, and sprinkler overspray adds more on lower panes. White spots that dry on glass in the sun can etch if left for months. You may notice a hazy look that does not wipe off. That is usually mineral buildup.

Spiders thrive near water because insects do, so webs gather on eaves and corners. Pollen is heavy in season, cedar from December to February and live oak in March and April. Pure water with near 0 TDS rinses glass without leaving new minerals behind. For old buildup we offer hard water spot removal at $10 per pane.

How we work Cardinal Hills Estates

No gate or HOA means we can book without extra steps. We reach upper and water side glass with poles from the ground, which matters on sloped waterfront yards where ladders are risky. Decks and docks need extra care, so we tell you our plan before starting and rinse where needed. Many waterfront owners add a house soft wash because humidity grows algae on siding and stucco. Patios and decks are cleaned by hand.

Sizes vary here, so we price by the house. A home of about 2,500 square feet would run near $250 for exterior windows at $10 per window, and a larger water view home near 3,500 square feet about $350. Allow roughly 45 to 60 minutes of exterior time for each 1,000 square feet.

Cardinal Hills Estates questions

Why does my glass look hazy even after wiping it?

That haze is often mineral deposit from lake spray or sprinklers. Regular wiping does not remove it. Hard water spot removal at $10 per pane can restore clarity if the glass is not etched.

Can you reach windows above a dock or steep slope?

Often yes. Poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we can stand on level ground and work upward. We tell you in advance if a spot looks unsafe.

Are you in this area often?

Yes. Lakeway is where we are based, and Tyler grew up here. We usually fit a new job in within about two weeks.

How much for a waterfront home?

Outside glass is priced at $10 a window, or about ten cents per square foot of home. Typical homes land near $350. Inside and out is $15 per window, and the minimum job is $200.

Will you clean the deck or dock?

We clean patios and decks by hand, and use a surface cleaner for flatwork, from $0.18 per square foot for concrete with a $250 minimum. We discuss docks case by case.
