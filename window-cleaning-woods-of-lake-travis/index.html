What Woods of Lake Travis homes are like

Lots here run about 1.3 acres, and as the name says, they are wooded. Homes range from roughly 3,000 to 3,650 sq ft, with prices from $875K to $1.2M. The neighborhood is not gated and shows no HOA, which leaves each owner in charge of the exterior.

The houses sit among oak and cedar, set back from the road and from each other. It feels more like Hill Country than lake front, with quiet streets and room to breathe, though the lake is not far.

Streets and landmarks: Trail of the Woods.

What Woods of Lake Travis windows deal with

Trees are the whole story. Leaves, twigs, sap, pollen and spider webs collect on the screens and in the window tracks, and shade holds moisture on frames so green growth starts earlier. Cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April coat everything in a yellow dust.

A shaded window does not look dirty from a distance, then a bright day shows the film. Pure water cleans the glass without leaving soap behind. Screens and tracks are where the hidden work is, at $5 each, and a full screen service is $10.

How we work Woods of Lake Travis

We start with the outside, using water fed poles so we can reach glass over shrubs and beds without stepping on them. Tree limbs can block a truck, so we ask about low branches on the drive. Because of the trees, a soft wash of the siding or roof often shows an obvious difference, and the gutters need attention too. Gutters cost $1.50 per linear foot for one story and $2.25 for two.

A 3,300 sq ft home comes to about $330 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft. We plan roughly two and a half to three and a half hours for the outside.

Woods of Lake Travis questions

My screens are always full of webs. Is that normal here?

On wooded lots, yes. Trees bring insects, webs and pollen, and they gather on screens and in tracks. Cleaning them regularly keeps windows opening smoothly and the glass looking cleaner for longer.

Do you remove sap from windows?

Light sap often comes off by hand. If sap has hardened, we look at it first and tell you whether it can be removed without scratching the glass.

Should I do gutters at the same time?

It is a good idea. Leaves and needles fill gutters on wooded lots, and combining services saves a trip. Gutter work is priced per linear foot: $1.50 for one story, $2.25 for two, $3.25 for three.

How often should a wooded home be cleaned?

Many homes under tree cover do well quarterly. Quarterly is 15% off and includes a free screen cleaning each year. Twice a Year at 10% off suits lighter cases.

Can the truck get down my drive?

Usually yes. If branches hang low or the drive is narrow, send a photo. We can park on the road and carry hoses to the house.
