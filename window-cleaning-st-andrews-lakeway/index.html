What St Andrews homes are like

These are smaller lock and leave homes next to the golf courses, from about 1,300 to 2,400 square feet. Prices run from $350K to $500K. St Andrews HOA manages the community, and there is no gate. Many owners use their homes part of the year, and some rent them out.

A lock and leave home sits closed for weeks. Glass can collect dust and pollen with nobody there to notice. A visit before you return makes the house feel cared for the day you arrive.

What St Andrews windows deal with

Golf courses use a lot of irrigation, and the spray carries minerals. If your windows face a fairway, you may see white spots on the lower panes after every watering cycle. Over the years those spots can etch. Cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April fall heavily on a house nobody is cleaning.

Garden homes often have patio doors, and the tracks fill with grit and spider webs. A home left shut for a stretch also gets webs in screens. Pure water with near 0 TDS dries clean, and hard water removal is $10 per pane.

How we work St Andrews

We can work while you are away if we can reach all the windows from outside. For interior cleaning, we arrange access with you or someone you trust. Poles reach upper windows from the ground. We follow HOA rules about parking and noise, and we avoid soaking patios, which we clean by hand. Owners who travel often pick Triannual or Quarterly so the cleaning is already on the calendar.

A 1,500 square foot home is about $150 for exterior windows, which falls under the $200 minimum, so the visit is $200. A 2,400 square foot home is about $240. Time on site is short, roughly an hour and a half to two hours outside.

St Andrews questions

Can you work while I am out of town?

Exterior windows, yes, as long as we can reach them. For inside work we need arranged access. Text us the dates you will be out and we plan around them.

Why do my windows have white spots near the golf course?

Course irrigation carries minerals that dry on glass. Regular cleaning prevents buildup, and set spots can be treated at $10 per pane.

Is the price different for condos?

No. The same rates apply, $10 per window, with a $200 minimum job. Many garden homes and condos land right at that minimum.

Do you offer a plan for part year owners?

Yes. Twice a Year takes 10% off, and Triannual takes 15% and adds a free screen cleaning each year.

Can you be here before I arrive for the season?

Usually, yes. Ask for a visit a week or two ahead and text us when your travel is firm. We cannot promise a specific day in advance, but we try to match your arrival.
