What Wildwood homes are like

Wildwood is a small subdivision with homes between about 1,400 and 2,000 square feet. Prices run around $600K to $900K, making it one of the more approachable corners of Bee Cave. No gate and no HOA are listed for Wildwood. Most houses have two or three bedrooms, one or two stories, and a back patio. Lots are modest, so yards are easy to walk. Being close to the shops and schools of Bee Cave is a big part of the appeal, and the homes here are the kind people keep tidy.

With fewer windows per house, a cleaning is a quick visit and fits easily into a morning.

What Wildwood windows deal with

Smaller homes still collect the same things. Cedar pollen in winter and live oak pollen in spring land on the glass, and the film can look gray after a rain. Sprinkler heads near the slab leave white mineral spots on ground floor windows, and those spots are what turn into permanent etching if no one removes them. Dust from roads and building sites nearby arrives all year. Sliding door tracks fill with grit, and screens hold webs and leaf bits, especially under trees.

Because the windows are smaller, spotting a streak is easy, so a pure water finish with no soap film makes a clear difference.

How we work Wildwood

With no gate and no HOA listed, scheduling is straightforward. We arrive, set up the pure water system and use water fed poles from the ground. One story homes do not need much reach, but the same system covers a second floor without a ladder against the siding. Indoors at Wildwood, panes are hand squeegeed over drop cloths. Because these homes are smaller, many owners combine the windows with screens and tracks. Neighbors who book together help us run the street in one visit, which also helps with the lead time, usually within two weeks.

A 1,700 square foot home would be about $170 for exterior windows by square footage, so the $200 minimum job applies. Exterior takes roughly an hour and a quarter to an hour and three quarters, and interior about 50 minutes.

Wildwood questions

My house is small. Is it still worth the call?

Yes. The minimum job is $200, and a home with 20 windows reaches it at $10 per window. Smaller homes are quick, and many owners add screens and tracks to get more from the visit.

How is the price worked out?

Exterior windows are $10 each ($5 per pane) or about $0.10 per square foot. Homes average about 10 windows per 1,000 square feet. Cleaning inside and out in Wildwood is $15 per window.

Can you do just the outside?

Yes. Exterior only is the most common request. Interior is an add on at the inside and out price, and it takes about 30 minutes per 1,000 square feet.

Do you remove spots from sprinklers?

Yes. Removing hard water spots in Wildwood costs $10 per pane. Plan members on higher tiers get it free on up to 5 windows each visit.
