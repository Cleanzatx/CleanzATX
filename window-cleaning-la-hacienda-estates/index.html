La Hacienda Estates is a small neighborhood on the Hudson Bend peninsula. It has both finished homes and open lots, with prices that run from about $400K up to $1M. It has no gate, and the listing record shows no HOA, so owners generally handle exterior upkeep themselves.

Because the area is mixed and still developing, you can see older homes beside newer ones on nearby lots. That variety is common around Hudson Bend, and it helps to remember when you ask for a quote, since the age and size of each house shape the work.

What La Hacienda Estates windows deal with

Being on the peninsula means the lake is never far. Hard water in the air and from sprinklers leaves white spots on glass, and in time those spots can etch. Wind and dust from Hudson Bend Road, open lots and nearby building work add a layer of grit, so windows here get dirty in a way that a quick rinse does not fix.

Older homes tend to have wood or aluminum frames with a lot of build up in the tracks. Spider webs and leaf litter also collect in the corners of screens. Track cleaning is $5, screens are $5, and hard water spot removal is $10 per pane.
