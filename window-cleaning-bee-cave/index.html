What Bee Cave windows deal with

Construction dust is the Bee Cave problem. Provence, Sweetwater, Madrone Ridge and the new phases along Hamilton Pool Rd are still being built, and the fine limestone dust from pads, road cuts and masonry work drifts onto every window downwind. It mixes with sprinkler overspray into a gray film that rain does not rinse off. New homes also come with brand new irrigation systems, and the hard water from those leaves white spotting on the lower panes within the first year. We treat set spots at $10 per pane, but on a newer home the goal is to clean often enough that they never get the chance to set at all.

The older neighborhoods have a different mix. Falconhead backs onto a golf course, so fairway irrigation spots the rear glass, and its early 2000s live oaks and cedars drop pollen from December through April along with leaf litter that packs the tracks. Belvedere and The Homestead sit in heavy cedar on hilltops where the wind brings dust and webs to every screen. Across all of it, the big rear windows and patio doors face west toward the Hill Country sunset, and that light exposes any soap film. Pure water from our dual RO, four stage system with a DI resin tank dries at near 0 TDS with nothing left on the glass.

Water fed poles reach three stories from the ground, so nothing leans on new stucco or stone.

A typical Bee Cave home runs 2,500 to 4,500 sq ft, so exterior window cleaning is about $250 to $450, with a 3,500 sq ft Falconhead or Sweetwater home about $350. At 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft that is roughly three hours outside; interiors add about 30 minutes per 1,000 sq ft. Screens are $5 and tracks $5 per window, and on a newer home we recommend both on the first visit to clear the grit. Driveways carry the same construction grit, so a concrete wash at $0.18 per sq ft pairs well, and limestone fronts get a soft wash, never pressure. Most Bee Cave jobs get scheduled within two weeks.

Bee Cave questions

What does window cleaning cost in Bee Cave?

Exterior glass is $10 per window, which works out to about $0.10 per sq ft of home, $200 minimum. A 3,500 sq ft home is about $350, and both sides run $15 per window.

Do you cover Belvedere and the Hamilton Pool Rd communities?

Yes. Belvedere, The Homestead, Provence, Rocky Creek, Ladera, Terra Colinas and Bella Colinas are all on our Hamilton Pool Rd route days, and each one has its own page further down.

My new build has a gray film on the glass. What is it?

Usually construction dust bonded with sprinkler minerals. A first thorough cleaning with pure water lifts the dust, and any spots that have already set are treated at $10 per pane.

Do you serve Spicewood addresses on TX 71?

The ones that touch Bee Cave, yes: West Cypress Hills, Travis Settlement, Deer Creek Ranch, Bee Creek Estates and the near side of Briarcliff. Dripping Springs is outside our area.

How often should Bee Cave windows be cleaned?

Quarterly while your neighborhood is still building, then Triannual once it settles down. Both plans are 15% off and include hard water removal on up to 5 windows each visit.

Can you wash the driveway on the same visit?

Yes. Driveway concrete runs $0.18 per sq ft, pavers $0.25, $250 minimum. We use a surface cleaner on flatwork and clean patios and pool decks by hand so they are not soaked.
