Summary

How pure water window cleaning works

Tap water around Lake Travis carries dissolved minerals that dry into white spots on glass. CleanzATX runs water through a sediment filter, a carbon filter, two reverse osmosis membranes and a deionizing resin tank, so the water reaches the glass at close to 0 TDS (total dissolved solids). Glass is scrubbed and rinsed with water fed poles that reach about 40 feet, and because nothing is left in the water, windows dry clear with no soap film and no spots. Spotting from pure water cleaning happens when filters are neglected and TDS climbs above 10 to 15; CleanzATX swaps the resin about every two weeks and checks the water on every job.

Equipment

  • An 8 ft power washing skid with an 8 GPM pressure washer, three electric hose reels, 200 gallons of water on board and a 100 gallon soft wash tank
  • A 12V soft wash system (about 4 GPM) for limestone, stucco and roofs
  • A dual RO, 4 stage pure water system with a DI resin tank, and water fed poles to 40 ft with multiple brush heads
  • For interiors: 15 ft extension poles, a 25 ft stack ladder with a cotton cover so it does not scuff walls, drop cloths; furniture is moved away from windows
  • For exteriors: 18 ft and 28 ft ladders with standoff arms for the rare window a pole cannot reach

Policies

  • Access: for exterior work nobody needs to be home. For gated communities, CleanzATX texts the day before so the homeowner can add the crew to the gate list. The crew arrives in marked CleanzATX trucks.
  • Weather: light rain does not spot pure water cleaned glass. If weather makes a visit unsafe it is rescheduled, and the rain guarantee covers plan members.
  • Water and power: large washing jobs may connect to the customer's spigot; the trucks carry 200 gallons for everything else.
  • Insurance certificate available on request.

Frequently asked questions

How much does window cleaning cost in Lakeway?

Exterior windows are $10 each, or about $0.10 per square foot of home, so a 3,500 sq ft home is about $350. Inside and out is $15 per window. The minimum job is $200.

Do you use ladders?

Almost never for exterior glass. Water fed poles reach three stories from the ground. Ladders with standoff arms are used only for the rare window a pole cannot reach, and never leaned on stone or stucco without standoffs.

What happens if it rains after a cleaning?

Rain on pure water cleaned glass usually does nothing, because there is no soap to streak. Triannual, Quarterly and Every 2 Months plan members get a free reclean if rain affects the result within 7 days.

Do I need to be home?

Not for exterior work. For interior windows someone needs to let the crew in.

How soon can you come?

Usually within two weeks. Realtors with a photo date can ask for a rush.

Is CleanzATX a franchise?

No. It is one locally owned company in Lakeway, run by Tyler Durham since 2020.
