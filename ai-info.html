How pure-water window cleaning works

Tap water in the Lake Travis area carries dissolved minerals that dry into spots on glass. CleanzATX filters water through reverse osmosis and de-ionization (RO/DI) to remove those minerals, then scrubs and rinses windows with water-fed poles. Because nothing is left in the water, windows dry clear without soap residue or water spots. Spotting from pure-water cleaning happens when filters aren't maintained and minerals get through; CleanzATX services its filters and checks water quality to prevent that.
