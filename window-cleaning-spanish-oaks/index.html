Spanish Oaks and the Bee Cave Rd estates

This hub covers Spanish Oaks itself and the large lot communities that share its stretch of TX 71 and Bee Cave Rd. Inside the Spanish Oaks gates are the Hillside, the double gated enclave of larger estate lots, the Villas at Spanish Oaks for lock and leave owners with golf access, and Barton Creek Preserve, acreage estates of three to twelve acres entered through the same gate. Madrone Canyon is the private gated community of 110 custom homesites next door on Twin Peaks Trace. The Uplands, about 150 homes on half acre to three acre lots inside the city of Bee Cave, sits where Bee Cave Rd meets TX 71.

East along Bee Cave Rd the hub takes in The Preserve at Barton Creek, about 100 custom homes on one to two acre lots off Preserve Dr; the Barton Creek community with its Fazio and Crenshaw courses around the Omni; Barton Creek West with its 400 acre private greenbelt and two pools; and Senna Hills, the late 1990s subdivision north of Bee Cave Rd. Down Cuernavaca Dr toward Lake Austin are Austin Lake Estates, Austin Lake Hills and Rivercrest, 1960s and 70s lake neighborhoods being rebuilt one lot at a time. What estate glass deals with here

Spanish Oaks homes are custom builds with walls of glass: floor to ceiling panes, clerestories above the great room, and multi slide doors onto the patio and pool. The square footage of glass per home is the highest we see anywhere, and most of it faces the fairways or the western ridge line, so it catches both golf course irrigation and the afternoon sun. Fairway sprinklers leave white mineral rings on the rear glass that etch over time, and we treat those at $10 per pane. Cedar is thick on these hilltops, so the pollen coat from December to February is heavy, and live oak pollen follows in March and April.

On the Bee Cave Rd side the problems shift with the age of the home. Barton Creek West and Senna Hills sit under mature canopy, so screens fill with webs and tracks with leaf litter every season. The Lake Austin neighborhoods off Cuernavaca get spray on water facing glass and a steady supply of construction dust from the rebuilds next door. Everywhere here, the glass is too tall and too expensive to risk soap film or a ladder. Pure water from our dual RO, four stage system with a DI resin tank dries at near 0 TDS and leaves nothing behind, and water fed poles reach three stories from the ground.

How we work Spanish Oaks

Spanish Oaks, Madrone Canyon, The Uplands and The Preserve at Barton Creek are all gated, and the Hillside sits behind a second gate inside the first. We ask you to call in our name or send the code the day before, and for Barton Creek Preserve the Spanish Oaks gatehouse handles it. From Lakeway it is a straight run down RM 620 to TX 71, so we pair Spanish Oaks with The Uplands and Madrone Canyon on one day and the Bee Cave Rd neighborhoods on another. No ladder touches the stone, stucco or standing seam metal; exterior glass runs off water fed poles from the ground, and interiors are hand squeegeed over drop cloths.

Homes in this hub run from 2,800 sq ft in The Uplands to well over 6,000 sq ft in Spanish Oaks and Barton Creek Preserve, so exterior window cleaning runs from about $280 to $600 and up. A typical 4,500 sq ft home is about $450 and takes three to four hours outside at 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft; interiors add about 30 minutes per 1,000 sq ft. Because the glass count is high, the Every 2 Months plan at 20% off makes sense for many owners here, with hard water removal on up to 5 windows each visit. Soft washing for limestone and gutter cleaning pair well on the same visit.
