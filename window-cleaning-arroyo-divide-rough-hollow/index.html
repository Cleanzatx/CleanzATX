What Arroyo Divide is like

Arroyo Divide belongs to the 2018 to 2019 phase of Rough Hollow, and homes are priced roughly $700K to $1.1M. They are production built, so floor plans repeat. There is no gate, and the Rough Hollow HOA is the only association.

Newer homes with repeating plans are easy for us. We know the window counts, the tall entry glass and the rear sliders, which makes quotes quick. Because the phase is only a few years old, owners are often first or second owners and haven't had the glass professionally cleaned yet.

What Arroyo Divide windows deal with

The first problem is leftover construction material. Paint specks, stucco splatter, labels and a hazy film can stay on new glass for years. Washing without care can scratch, so we scrape gently and finish with pure water.

The second is hard water. Fresh landscaping means fresh irrigation, and sprinklers spray the lower windows. Spots build up within weeks and can etch. A hard water treatment is $10 per pane. Some owners use film or tint, and we always ask before we scrape anything near it.

How we work Arroyo Divide

This section pairs naturally with Highland Terrace and Canyon Pass, so we often do several homes in a morning. Exterior work uses the poles from the ground, and any ladder work inside is done with cotton covered ladders and drop cloths.

A 3,000 sq ft home runs about $300 outside and 90 minutes more inside. If you want the sprinkler overspray to stop leaving spots, we can also show you where the heads hit. Quotes arrive by text, usually inside an hour. Add a quick patio hand clean if you like, and we handle both in one trip.

Arroyo Divide questions

Can you remove paint from my new windows?

Light paint specks can usually be removed with a careful scrape and no harm to the glass. We check first and avoid anything that risks scratching.

Why are my newer windows already spotted?

Sprinklers. New landscaping runs often and the water is hard. Regular cleaning prevents etching.

How do you price a production home?

At $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft. A 3,000 sq ft home is about $300 outside.

Do you offer plans?

Yes. The Clean Windows Club has Twice a Year, Triannual, Quarterly and Every 2 Months plans, saving 10% to 20%.

Does it matter if my windows have a tint or film?

Yes, we like to know ahead. We avoid scraping anywhere near tinted or filmed glass, and we use a gentler method.

How often do new homes need cleaning?

Twice a year is common, with extra visits if sprinklers hit the glass.
