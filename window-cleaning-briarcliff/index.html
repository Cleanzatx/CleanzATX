What Briarcliff homes are like

The Village of Briarcliff has roughly 1,000 or more homes, and prices stretch from about $400K to $2M, so the range is wider than most places we work. It has a Briarcliff POA, its own marina and Willie Nelson's Cut N Putt course. Some houses are weekend retreats and some are lived in year round, which means some stay shut for weeks at a time. There is no gate. Streets wind over the hills toward the water, and many homes are built to capture lake views with decks and walls of glass. We serve the part of the village nearest Bee Cave and Lakeway.

That mix gives Briarcliff more variety of homes than any single builder subdivision.

What Briarcliff windows deal with

Lake views mean lake conditions. Spray and humidity from Lake Travis leave mineral films, and sprinklers add hard water scale to ground level windows. West facing glass looks into low afternoon sun, and every streak shows. Cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in spring coat the glass, and homes that sit empty during the week build up more dust and webs than occupied ones. Decks and rails catch dust that gets tracked against the glass.

Screens and sliding door tracks fill with grit and insects, so they benefit from a regular clean.

How we work Briarcliff

No gate to arrange, but hills and tight streets mean we check access first, and we carry our own 200 gallons so there is no need for your hose. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, which suits decks and tall lake side walls where a ladder would be unsafe. Indoors at Briarcliff, panes are hand squeegeed over drop cloths. For weekend homes, tell us your visit dates and give us access instructions, and we can clean while you are away. Pairing the windows with a soft wash on siding and a gutter cleaning is common, since lake humidity encourages algae.

A 2,400 square foot home is about $240 for exterior windows. Plan on a bit under two hours outside and roughly an hour inside.
