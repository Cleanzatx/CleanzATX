What Barton Creek homes are like

Public records describe about 4,000 acres, 19 neighborhoods and thousands of homes, with four property owners associations and partly gated sections. Prices range from about $1M to $10M. That range means everything from golf course homes to hillside estates, and the age, style and window count change from one street to the next.

We do not list every sub neighborhood here. If you live in Barton Creek, the best step is to text your address, and we quote your home on its actual size and glass.

What Barton Creek windows deal with

Fairway homes face irrigation overspray, which leaves mineral spots on the glass nearest the course. Hillside homes get afternoon sun on west facing walls that shows every streak, and the many mature oaks bring pollen, leaf litter and webs to screens and tracks.

Hill Country water is hard, so a sprinkler hitting a window for a season can leave a film that normal cleaning never touches. We remove it at $10 per pane and then use pure water to stop it returning.

Barton Creek questions

Do you serve all of Barton Creek?

We serve homes across the community, though access varies by section. Text your address, the gate code if there is one, and any association rules, and we confirm whether we can schedule you.

How much does a Barton Creek home cost?

The outside glass is $10 per window, or about $0.10 per sq ft of house. For a 4,000 square foot home that lands near $400. Both sides is $15 per window, with a $200 minimum job.

Can you remove sprinkler spots from golf course homes?

Yes, at $10 per pane for removal. After that, our water near 0 TDS keeps glass from picking up more film, and regular visits stop it building again.

Do you clean glass up high on tall homes?

Yes. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so no ladder rests against the house for exterior glass. Interior ladders have cotton covers and we lay drop cloths.

What if I am not sure my section lets contractors in?

Ask your association and send us what they say. We follow gate and hours rules, and we will not schedule until the access is clear and you have confirmed the quote.
