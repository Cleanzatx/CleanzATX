What Vista Ridge is like

Vista Ridge came in during the 2018 to 2019 building phase, so most homes here are only a few years old. The lots are oversized for Rough Hollow, the sites are on a hill, and many of the houses are custom builds designed around a lake view. Prices run roughly $900K to $2M, and the section is not gated.

The larger lots mean longer walks around the house. Side yards, rear patios and upper decks are all part of the glass line, and the homes tend to run tall so the view clears the neighbor. Plan on a walkaround before we start, so every window, door light and transom is counted and priced up front.

What Vista Ridge windows deal with

A home that is only a few years old still has stucco dust and fine grit settled on the glass, especially while neighboring lots are being finished. Wind on the hilltop carries it around. The lake view glass is also usually the tallest and widest on the house, which shows streaks in late sun.

Sprinkler water from the large lawns lands on lower panes and leaves white spots. Hard water removal runs $10 per pane when we find mineral buildup. Cedar and oak pollen collect in screens and tracks, so a track clean at $5 each is a good add on.

How we work Vista Ridge

The Rough Hollow HOA covers this section, and we keep our work within its normal expectations: quiet, tidy, off the street when possible. Because Vista Ridge is close to Vista Ridge Estates, we often schedule neighbors the same morning. House soft wash for stucco and a hand cleaned patio are common pairings.

A custom home of 4,500 sq ft is about $450 for exterior glass and takes around three and a half hours outside. Inside is another two hours or so. A Quarterly plan takes 15% off and brings the yearly free screen clean and the 7 day rain guarantee.

Vista Ridge questions

Are Vista Ridge homes new enough for builder residue?

Many are a few years old, and some neighboring lots are still finishing, so fine dust and occasional paint specks are common. We handle them gently and finish with pure water.

Can you reach the second and third story view windows?

Yes. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so no ladders touch the house.

Do you clean the lake facing sliders as well?

Yes, sliders and their tracks are part of the job. Tracks are $5 each and we vacuum and wipe them out.

How is Vista Ridge different from Vista Ridge Estates?

Estates is the larger lot version. The glass jobs are similar, but estate homes often run bigger and take longer. We quote each by square footage.

Do you clean door glass and transoms?

Yes. Entry door lights, sidelights and the transom windows above the doors are counted as windows and priced at the same $10 each. Tall entry glass is reached with the poles from the ground.
