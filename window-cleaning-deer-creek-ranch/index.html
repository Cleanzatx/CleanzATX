What Deer Creek Ranch homes are like

Deer Creek Ranch is a larger, spread out subdivision with several hundred homes on acreage lots, priced at around $500K to $1.2M. A 33 acre private park with lake privileges is the signature feature, with a voluntary Parks and Lakes HOA behind it. There is no gate. The mix is wide: ranch style homes from earlier years sit near newer builds, and some lots are wooded while others are open. Twin Lake Hills is part of this area, with the same acreage character.

Many owners here chose the area for space and quiet, and that shows in the large yards and screened porches.

What Deer Creek Ranch windows deal with

Rural acreage brings the dust, pollen and bugs that come with open ground. Cedar pollen collects from December to February, live oak pollen in spring, and wind carries grass seed and dirt over the lots all year. Many homes use well water or heavy irrigation, so white mineral spots on lower windows are common, and long running sprinklers can start to etch glass. Mature trees near the house bring leaf litter and spider webs to screens and tracks. Storms push mud and splatter onto lower panes.

Screened porches and sliding doors add more glass than most owners count, and they gather the most grime.

How we work Deer Creek Ranch

No gate, so we drive in, though some lots sit down long gravel drives. Our skid holds 200 gallons of water on board, which means we do not need yours. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we keep ladders off the siding. Indoors at Deer Creek Ranch, panes are hand squeegeed over drop cloths. Since lots are far apart, we ask neighbors to book the same week when they can, which helps us group stops. Ranch style homes pair well with a house soft wash, a roof soft wash for algae under trees, and gutters.
