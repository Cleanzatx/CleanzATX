What Hills of Lakeway homes are like

The Hills is one of the bigger gated communities around Lake Travis. It has 600+ homes spread over several plat phases, numbered Hills of Lakeway Phase 1 through 9 and beyond. Homes run from about 2,000 to 7,000 square feet, and prices go from the high $400Ks up to around $2.7M. You can read the age of the place in the houses. Some date to the 1980s, with the stone and stucco of that era. Others are new builds with big walls of glass. A Hills Country Club social membership comes with the home, so plenty of owners spend their days on the course side of the property.

Because the community went up in phases over decades, no two streets feel quite the same. Mature live oaks and cedars shade the older sections, while newer phases have younger landscaping and more open sky. The ground is hilly, so many homes sit above the street with long driveways and glass pointed at the views. That mix is why we quote from the real size of your home and not from a flat rate.

What Hills windows deal with

Golf course irrigation is the first thing we see. Overspray from the sprinklers lands on glass facing the fairways and dries into white mineral spots, and after a few seasons it can etch the glass. Our pure water system is a dual RO, 4 stage setup with a DI resin tank, so the water is near 0 TDS and the glass dries with no spots and no soap film. Add cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April, and a window can look dull in a week. Big view windows on the back of the house show every streak in afternoon sun.

Older phases often have trees close to the walls, which means spider webs, leaf litter in the screens and grit in the tracks. Screens run $5 each and tracks $5, and hard water spot removal in The Hills of Lakeway is $10 per pane when minerals have set in. Tall newer glass is easier than it looks, because our water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground at The Hills of Lakeway.
