What Sweetwater homes are like

Sweetwater covers about 1,400 acres, with 700 of them kept as open space and roughly ten miles of trails. There are three pools, the Sweetwater Club and the Overlook Club, and building began in the mid 2010s and has not stopped. Eventually it will hold more than 2,000 homes. Houses run from 1,500 to 7,700 square feet, with a median price near $685,000, and the range spans the upper $300s to over $1 million. The Sweetwater HOA covers all of it, plus a MUD, and two sections are gated.

Most homes are stucco and limestone in the Hill Country style, many with covered patios facing green belts or trails.

What Sweetwater windows deal with

The first thing is construction. Because new phases are still going in, dust from grading, concrete and framing blows across streets that are already lived in. It settles on glass, frames and sills, and a bit of morning dew turns it into a stubborn film. If your own yard is newly landscaped, expect more dust and also irrigation spots, because new sprinkler heads tend to hit the walls.

The wide open space brings pollen, cedar in winter and oak in spring, and the limestone dust that is native to this part of Travis County. Our pure water system rinses glass clean without soap film. Stucco and limestone get a soft wash with the 12V system, since pressure on stone causes damage.

Sweetwater questions

Why are my new windows still dirty after move in?

Builder dust, stucco residue and sprinkler spots stay behind. A first cleaning with pure water, and hard water removal where needed, clears them.

Do you service the gated sections?

Yes. Send the gate code or contact in your quote text and we confirm the day before. Davenport Summit and The Summit at Sweetwater are the two gated sections.

How often should I clean with construction nearby?

Quarterly works well for many homes while building continues nearby. The plan saves 15% and includes a yearly free screen cleaning.

What about my patio and pool deck?

Yes. Patios and pool decks are cleaned by hand so they are not soaked, and driveways use a surface cleaner at $0.18 per square foot for concrete.
