What the Villas are like

The Villas at Rough Hollow are a townhome and villa community inside Rough Hollow, priced around $600K to $1M. They are lock and leave homes, and have their own association alongside the master HOA. The community is not gated.

Villa life means less yard and more attention to shared walls and common areas. Windows tend to be fewer than on a detached estate, but they are carefully placed for light and privacy. Most villas share a wall, so there are windows on only two or three sides.

What Villa windows deal with

Courtyard and patio glass collects dust, pollen and bird droppings with no one to wipe it. Pollen from cedar and oak sits on the sills. When units stand empty, webs and dust settle.

Small lawns and planter beds are watered with sprinklers close to the walls, so hard water spots appear on the lower panes. We treat them at $10 per pane. We keep the water off shared walkways. Birds like the quiet of a courtyard, and bird droppings are a regular find on sills and rails.

How we work the Villas

We confirm association rules first, including where to park and when work is allowed. Poles reach the upper floors, and patios are cleaned by hand, which keeps water from running into neighbors' space. Window cleaning is the main service here, with a patio hand clean as an easy add on.

A 2,500 sq ft villa is about $250 for exterior windows and about two hours outside. Interior adds about 75 minutes. A Triannual plan takes 15% off, and plans keep visits coming without you remembering.
