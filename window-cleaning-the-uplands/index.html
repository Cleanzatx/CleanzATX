What The Uplands homes are like

The Uplands has about 150 homes on oversized lots, from half an acre to three acres, in Lake Travis ISD. Listings show homes of 2,800 to 5,678 square feet and prices from $1.1M to $2.2M. That makes it a mid sized estate neighborhood with a lot of two story homes, large entry windows, and stone and stucco exteriors.

It is gated and run by the Uplands HOA. Lots are big enough that many homes sit well back from the road, and mature oaks and cedar fill the spaces between them.

What Uplands windows deal with

Trees are the main story here. Oak and cedar bring pollen to the glass from December through April, and leaf litter, spider webs and dust gather in screens and tracks all year. Homes with large west facing glass show every streak in the afternoon sun, which is when pure water drying without spots matters most.

Sprinkler overspray on big lots lands on lower panes and dries into white mineral spots. Left alone for a few seasons it can etch the glass, so we treat existing spots at $10 per pane and then keep them off with regular visits.

The Uplands questions

Is The Uplands gated for service visits?

Yes, it is gated. Send us the gate code or the contact to call when you request a quote, and we plan the visit around it. Please share any HOA contractor rules as well.

How much does a typical Uplands home cost?

Outside glass is $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft, so a 3,500 square foot house is near $350. Both sides is $15 per window. The minimum job is $200.

Why do my windows look streaky in the afternoon?

West facing glass shows soap film and mineral residue in low sun. Our pure water leaves no film, so panes dry clear. Heavy existing deposit is treated with hard water removal at $10 per pane.

Can I combine windows with a house wash?

Yes. House soft wash starts at $0.20 per sq ft, $400 minimum. Many Uplands owners add the driveway at $0.18 per sq ft for concrete, and we do it all on one visit.

Do you offer a plan for Uplands homeowners?

The Clean Windows Club has Twice a Year at 10% off, Triannual and Quarterly at 15% off and Every 2 Months at 20% off. Plans include the 7 day rain guarantee on higher tiers.
