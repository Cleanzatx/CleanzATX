How Rough Hollow is laid out

Rough Hollow is a master planned community with 21 to 22 sections and several thousand lots at build out, though the exact count is not published. It runs along about three miles of Lake Travis shoreline, and the main road through it is Highlands Blvd. Most of the amenities sit at two points: Highland Village, the hub with the lazy river, and the Rough Hollow Yacht Club and marina, which has 294 or more slips. Homes are mostly stucco and stone, in a Mediterranean or Hill Country modern style, and the community is still being built.

The sections fall into rough groups. The lakefront group is The Peninsula, The Point, Waters Edge and Lakeside. The hilltop group is The Bluffs, Vista Ridge, West Rim, East Rim, The Overlook and The Summit. Canyon Pass, Highland Terrace, Cypress Ridge and the rest sit in between, and the townhome and villa pockets are their own thing.

Streets and landmarks: Highlands Blvd, Highland Village, Rough Hollow Yacht Club.

Lakefront and hilltop glass are different jobs

On the shoreline the problem is spray and sun. Lake water is hard, sprinklers add more, and west facing glass over the water shows every streak after about three in the afternoon. Left alone, those white spots can etch into the glass. Pure water at near 0 TDS dries without leaving anything behind, and a hard water treatment handles older spotting.

Up on the ridges the glass is bigger and taller. Hilltop homes tend to have large view windows, second floor sliders and three story walls of glass facing the lake. Our water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so no ladders touch the house. Wind carries limestone dust and construction dust up there too, since new lots are still opening.

Rough Hollow questions

Which Rough Hollow sections do you clean?

Nearly all of them, including The Peninsula, The Point, The Bluffs, Vista Ridge, Canyon Pass and the villa and townhome pockets. If you are unsure which section you live in, send a text with your street and we will place it.

Do you get through the gates?

Yes. The Peninsula and The Enclave are gated, so we set up access with you before the visit. You tell us how you want us let in and we plan the stop around it.

Can you reach the tall lake view windows?

Exterior glass up to three stories is reached from the ground with water fed poles, so there are no ladders against the house. Interior glass on two story rooms is done by hand with cotton covered ladders and drop cloths.

How often should Rough Hollow homes be cleaned?

Lakefront homes do well on a quarterly plan because of spray and sun. Hilltop and inland homes are fine twice or three times a year. The Clean Windows Club saves 10% to 20% depending on the plan.
