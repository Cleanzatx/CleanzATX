What Shepherd Mountain is like

Shepherd Mountain climbs a Hill Country slope south of RM 2222 and west of Loop 360, with Lake Austin close by. It holds several hundred homes, built between 1989 and 2015, on lots from about a quarter acre to more than four acres. That spread means you find small lots on tight streets and big acreage homes up the hill. Prices for houses run about $1M to $2.2M, and Shepherd Mountain HOA covers the area. There are condos here too, a separate group we handle on their own page. The streets twist with the terrain, and some driveways are steep.

With homes built across 25 years, styles range from early Hill Country stone to newer contemporary plans.

What Shepherd Mountain windows deal with

Hillside homes are tall on the downhill side. A house that looks like two stories from the street can be three from the back, with glass on every level. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we do not need to set ladders on a slope, which is where ladder work gets risky. Interior stairwell windows get cotton covered ladders and hand work.

Sprinklers on sloped lawns run hard water onto the lower panes, and wind brings dust uphill. Cedar pollen peaks December to February, then live oak pollen in March and April. Our dual RO, 4 stage system with a DI resin tank gives near 0 TDS water so glass dries without spots. Screens are $5 each, tracks are $5, and hard water removal is $10 per pane.

How we work Shepherd Mountain

Steep driveways and tight turns mean we park carefully, sometimes on the street, and walk the gear in. We text when we are close and confirm where you want the truck. Shepherd Mountain is not gated. Exterior glass goes first, then screens and tracks, then interior by hand with squeegees and drop cloths if you book inside and out. Hillside runoff also puts mud and grit onto walls and patios, so house soft wash at $0.20 per sq ft and flatwork cleaning at $0.18 per sq ft for concrete often pair with windows. Patios are cleaned by hand.

A 4,000 sq ft hillside home runs about $400 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft. Count on roughly three to four hours outside and two hours inside, with a $200 minimum job.

Shepherd Mountain questions

Do you clean both houses and condos on Shepherd Mountain?

Yes. Houses use the home price of about $0.10 per sq ft for exterior windows. Condos have their own page with the $200 minimum job explained.

Is it safe to work on a steep lot?

Yes, because exterior glass is cleaned from the ground with water fed poles. We keep ladders off slopes and use them inside only, with cotton covers to protect surfaces.

Where do you park?

On the driveway if there is room, otherwise on the street. We text before arriving and ask where you prefer the truck, then carry the gear in on foot.

Do lake views mean more cleaning?

Lake views mean big glass, and big glass shows every spot. Pure water dries clear, so view windows look good longer. Hard water removal is $10 per pane for any set spotting.
