What Lakewood Hills homes are like

Most Lakewood Hills homes date from the mid 2000s and are on the larger side, from about 3,200 to 5,600 sq ft, with prices around $810K to $1.65M. The section is gated and has its own sub association, and owners get Lake Club access. A few lots have views down toward Lake Austin, which is where the sunsets and the glare come from. The builds are typical of that era in Steiner Ranch: stone and stucco fronts, tall entries, two story great rooms with plenty of glass, and covered back patios.

With fewer than a hundred homes, the streets are quiet, and you can tell which owners keep up with maintenance.

Streets and landmarks: Lakewood Hills Terrace.

What Lakewood Hills windows deal with

Homes of this age have had fifteen or more years of sprinkler spray on the glass, and that is the biggest problem we see. Hard water leaves white spots that bond to the surface, and left alone they can etch. Pure water cleaning handles most of it, and the stubborn panes get hard water spot removal at $10 each. The lake view side faces west in many cases, so afternoon sun shows every streak and film. Cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April coat everything, and the tree cover brings webs and leaf bits into screens and tracks.

Older caulk and weatherstrip are worth a glance, and we will tell you if we see something.

How we work Lakewood Hills

We confirm gate access before the visit, and we will not show up unannounced. Exterior glass is cleaned with our dual RO, four stage pure water system with a DI tank, so it dries with no soap film, and poles reach up to three stories without ladders on the stone. Inside, we clean by hand with squeegees and put down drop cloths first. Because Lakewood Hills sits close to other gated Steiner Ranch sections, we can often schedule neighbors on the same day, and most visits happen within two weeks. Many owners add a house soft wash, since stucco and limestone only get soft washed here, never pressure washed.

At $0.10 per sq ft, a 4,000 sq ft Lakewood Hills home runs about $400 on the outside. Expect roughly three to four hours outside and two hours inside.

Lakewood Hills questions

Do you handle the gate and sub association?

We just need the gate code or guest list name. We do not need anything from the association. Text us the details when you book and we will arrive when we said we would.

My windows have white spots that will not wipe off. Can you fix that?

Often, yes. Spot removal on a stubborn pane costs $10. If a pane looks etched we will say so before we start, since etching is damage to the surface and not just dirt.

Can you clean the lake view windows well enough to see the water?

Yes. We clean with pure water so there is no soap film, and we dry check the glass in angled light, which is where west facing lake glass shows streaks first.

How much for a typical Lakewood Hills home?

Exterior windows are about $0.10 per sq ft, so 4,000 sq ft is about $400. Adding the inside brings Lakewood Hills homes to $15 per window. Minimum job is $200, and quotes come by text.

Can you soft wash my stucco and roof too?

Yes. House soft wash starts at $0.20 per sq ft with a $400 minimum, and roof soft wash starts at $0.25 per sq ft with a $400 minimum. We use a 12V soft wash system, not high pressure.
