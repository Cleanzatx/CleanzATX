What Bellagio Estates homes are like

This is the quiet end of Steiner Ranch. Roughly 30 estate sites sit behind a private gate off Flat Top Ranch Road, and the homes are the largest custom builds in the whole community, from about 5,000 to 8,000 sq ft. Prices run from around $2M to $4M. Lots are generous, the architecture leans toward stone, stucco and big rooflines, and there is often a lot of glass at entries, stairwells and the main living area. Nobody drives through unless they live here or are visiting someone who does.

A neighborhood this small has its own sub association, and owners usually like service providers who show up on time, finish, and leave things tidy.

Streets and landmarks: Flat Top Ranch Road.

What Bellagio Estates windows deal with

Large homes mean large panes and many of them. A single 6,000 sq ft house can have well over fifty windows, so any film or spotting is easy to see. Stone and stucco shed fine dust onto sills and glass. Sprinklers aimed near the house leave hard water spots, and on big picture windows that can etch if it sits, so we remove it at $10 per pane. Tall entries and stair towers need real reach, and that is where water fed poles earn their keep, since they reach up to three stories from the ground.

Spring pollen is heavy on large lots with mature trees, and screens and tracks fill up with it and with leaf bits.

How we work Bellagio Estates

We plan these as longer visits. We confirm gate access before we arrive, arrive within the window we agreed, and work through the house in order so nothing is rushed. Exterior glass is cleaned with pure water from a dual RO, four stage system, so it dries clear with no soap film. Interiors are cleaned by hand with squeegees, with drop cloths down and cotton covered ladders on two story rooms. At this size, owners often pair windows with a house soft wash from $0.20 per sq ft, which uses the 12V soft wash system, and a gentle roof clean from $0.25 per sq ft.

A 5,000 sq ft home is about $500 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft. Count on roughly four to five hours outside and about two and a half hours inside.

Bellagio Estates questions

How long does a house this size take?

For exterior windows, we plan about 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft, so a 5,000 sq ft house is roughly four to five hours. Interior glass runs about 30 minutes per 1,000 sq ft.

Do you need gate access to Bellagio Estates?

Yes. Send us the gate code or have us on the guest list, and we will text when we are on the way. We leave the property closed and tidy when we finish.

Can you reach the tall entry windows?

Yes. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so tall entries and stair glass get cleaned from the outside without a ladder touching your stucco or stone.

What would I pay for a large home?

Exterior windows are $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft, so a 5,000 sq ft home is about $500. Adding the inside brings Bellagio Estates homes to $15 per window. Screens are $5 and tracks are $5.
