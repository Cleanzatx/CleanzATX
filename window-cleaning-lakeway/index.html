The Lakeway neighborhoods we cover

Lakeway runs along the south shore of Lake Travis between RM 620 and the water, and this page covers the whole city plus the 78734 and 78738 addresses that share its roads. Old Lakeway is the original plat, the numbered sections around Lakeway Marina and the Live Oak golf course. Rough Hollow fills the south side with more than twenty sections of stucco and stone homes above three miles of shoreline. Serene Hills sits on 456 acres off Serene Hills Dr with most of its land left as preserve. Lakeway Highlands and Flintrock Falls hold the newer custom builds toward The Hills, and Bella Montagna is the gated acre lot pocket on the city line.

Between those big names are dozens of smaller subdivisions we work just as often. Cardinal Hills and Cardinal Hills Estates sit off RM 620 toward Hudson Bend. The Ridge at Alta Vista and Alta Vista wrap around Lohmans Crossing. North Lakeway Village and the Preserve at Lakeway cover the north side, and Lohmans Crossing Estates, Round Mountain and Tuscan Village cluster near Lakeway Regional. What Lakeway windows deal with

In Lakeway, cedar pollen rolls through from December to February and live oak pollen follows in March and April, leaving a yellow film that rain only smears. Mature live oaks in Old Lakeway drop leaf litter into tracks and give spiders a roof to work under, so screens fill with webs by midsummer. On the newer side of town, Rough Hollow and Lakeway Highlands are still building, and construction dust settles on every pane downwind of a job site. Lakefront homes on the Hurst Creek arm get lake spray on the water side and sprinkler overspray on the street side, and both leave white mineral spots that etch over a summer.

West and southwest facing glass is the giveaway in this town. Most view homes are built to look at the lake and the sunset, so the biggest panes face straight into the afternoon sun, and that light shows every streak, every trace of soap residue and every hard water ring. That is why we clean with pure water instead of soap. Our dual RO, four stage system with a DI resin tank brings the water to near 0 TDS, so it dries clear with nothing left behind. For spots that have already set in, hard water removal is $10 per pane, and most Lakeway homes only need it on the windows nearest a sprinkler head.

Window cleaning pairs well here with gutter cleaning in the fall, once the oaks have dropped, and a driveway or patio wash in spring after the pollen is gone. Lakeway questions

How much does window cleaning cost in Lakeway?

Exterior windows are $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft of home, with a $200 minimum. Inside and out is $15 per window. A typical Lakeway home is about $350.

Do you cover Rough Hollow and Serene Hills too?

Yes. Rough Hollow, Serene Hills, Lakeway Highlands, Flintrock Falls, Old Lakeway and every smaller subdivision between them are on our regular route, and each one has its own page below.

Can you reach the high windows on a three story home?

Yes. Water fed poles clean exterior glass up to three stories from the ground, so no ladder touches the house. Two story interiors get covered ladders, drop cloths and squeegees.

What are the white spots on my lake side windows?

Mineral deposits from lake spray and sprinklers. Normal cleaning will not lift them once set. We treat them at $10 per pane, and plans at Triannual and above include 5 windows a visit.

How often should Lakeway windows be cleaned?

Three or four times a year suits most homes here: after cedar season, after oak pollen, and before the holidays. Triannual and Quarterly plans are 15% off, Every 2 Months 20% off.

What if it rains right after you clean?

Rain on pure water cleaned glass usually dries clean because there is no soap film to streak. If anything looks off within 7 days, text a photo and we return free.
