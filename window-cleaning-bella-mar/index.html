What Bella Mar homes are like

Bella Mar is built on the rolling ground of Steiner Ranch, and many lots back to a canyon or a greenbelt. Homes run from about 2,500 to 6,500 sq ft, with prices around $800K to $1.5M. That range means a mix of well kept family houses and some big custom looking homes with tall entries and wide rear windows. The section has its own amenity center and pools, and the streets curve through low hills, so you see a lot of rear elevations that look out over open space instead of another house. It is not gated, and the Steiner Ranch Master Association covers the section.

People here tend to care about the view, which is the right instinct. The glass is how you see it.

What Bella Mar windows deal with

Open greenbelt and canyon backs bring a few things you do not get on a plain street. Cedar and live oak pollen drift in from the trees, and wind carries dust across open ground. Spider webs gather in corners, on eaves and in screen frames, especially where lots touch native brush. Sprinkler spray on the sides of the house leaves hard water spots that can etch glass if left alone, and we remove those at $10 per pane. Rear glass that faces west can look fine in the morning and streaky by evening, so we check it in angled light before we leave.

Screens and tracks collect leaf litter and webs, so adding those makes the whole window look new.

How we work Bella Mar

Bella Mar is open, so access is easy. We park on the street, and if you want us to reach a rear yard we ask that gates are unlocked and pets are inside. Pure water from our dual RO system with a DI tank dries with no spots, and water fed poles reach up to three stories, so we never need a ladder on the siding. Inside windows are done by hand. Many Bella Mar owners also add a house soft wash or a patio and pool deck clean, since patios are cleaned by hand so the deck does not get soaked. We route these jobs together with other Steiner Ranch sections, usually within two weeks.

For a typical 3,500 sq ft home, exterior windows are about $350 at $0.10 per sq ft. Expect about two and a half to three and a half hours outside and around an hour and three quarters for the interior.

Bella Mar questions

Can you clean tall rear windows that face the canyon?

Yes. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so exterior glass is cleaned without a ladder against the house. We also dry check west facing glass in afternoon light so streaks do not show later.

Do you handle spider webs on greenbelt lots?

We knock down webs around windows, frames and eaves as part of the exterior clean. Screens and tracks can be added at $5 each, which clears out the leaf litter and dust that builds up near native brush.

Is there anything to arrange with the association?

Bella Mar is not gated and falls under the Steiner Ranch Master Association. We do not need anything from them. Just make sure rear gates are unlocked and pets are inside on the day.

What is a typical price in Bella Mar?

Exterior windows are $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft, so a 3,500 sq ft home is about $350. Doing inside and out costs $15 per window, and no Bella Mar job runs below $200.

Can you also wash my pool deck?

Yes. Patios and pool decks are cleaned by hand so they do not get soaked, at $0.18 per sq ft for concrete and $0.25 for pavers, with a $250 minimum.
