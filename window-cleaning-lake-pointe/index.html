What Lake Pointe homes are like

Lake Pointe is a family neighborhood of about 825 homes in unincorporated Travis County, just outside Bee Cave, with an Austin 78738 mailing address. Lots run from a quarter acre to a full acre and prices start around $500,000 and reach beyond $1.5 million. The community has a private park with a swim dock and an LCRA day dock on Lake Austin, a pool, a splash pad, and Lake Pointe Elementary within the neighborhood.

The Lake Pointe HOA oversees the whole area, with one gated section, The Pointe. The rest is open. Sections include The Setting, The Landing, The Estates and Vista Pointe, and each has a slightly different age and feel.

What Lake Pointe windows deal with

Being near the water changes the glass. Spray and humidity off Lake Austin leave mineral deposits, and lawn sprinklers do the same on every lot. White spots on lower panes are the first thing owners notice. If they stay for years, hard water can etch the glass. West facing windows toward the lake or the hills show every streak in afternoon sun.

Trees on larger lots drop webs and leaf bits into screens and tracks, and the oak pollen season in March and April gives everything a yellow layer. Stucco and limestone on these homes are soft washed with our 12V system, never pressure washed.

How we work Lake Pointe

We run Lake Pointe by section so the route stays tight. The gated Pointe needs a code, and we ask for it when we quote. The larger lots sometimes mean a long walk from the street, which our pole system handles without ladders against the wall. Our poles do the reaching from the ground, up to three stories. Families here often choose a Quarterly or Triannual plan, which takes 15% off and includes hard water removal on up to 5 windows each visit.

For a 3,500 square foot house, exterior windows are about $350 and take roughly two and a half to three and a half hours. A house soft wash starts at $0.20 per square foot, with a $400 minimum.

Lake Pointe questions

Does lake spray cause the spots on my glass?

It can contribute. Mineral rich water from the lake or sprinklers dries into white spots on glass near the water. Hard water removal runs $10 per pane.

Do you service every section of Lake Pointe?

Yes. That includes The Setting, The Landing, The Estates, Vista Pointe and the gated Pointe. We keep the same pricing for all of them.

Will you work around my children and pets?

We do. Tell us about dogs in the yard or a nap schedule when you text, and we plan around it and keep gates latched.

Is a pole system really as good as a ladder?

Usually better. Pure water at near 0 TDS rinses clean, the poles reach three stories, and we skip the ladder marks on walls and landscaping.

Can you clean my screens as well?

Yes. Standard screens are $5, full screens $10, and solar screens $15. Triannual, Quarterly and Every 2 Months plans include a free screen cleaning each year.
