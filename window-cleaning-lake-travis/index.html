Window Cleaning Conditions Around Lake Travis

Lake Travis is the reservoir on the Colorado River formed by Mansfield Dam, and its shoreline winds past Lakeway, Hudson Bend, Volente, Point Venture and other communities. Homes along it are built to face the water, which usually means large windows, sliding glass doors and upper-floor balconies on the lake side.

Afternoon sun off the water makes every spot and streak on west-facing glass easy to see. Lake water and tap water around Lake Travis are both hard, so sprinklers, hose rinses and lake spray leave mineral deposits that, left too long, can etch into the glass.

Patios, decks and dock areas near the water also collect pollen, spider webs and grime. We can power wash those on the same visit as your windows, so the whole lake side of the house gets done at once.
