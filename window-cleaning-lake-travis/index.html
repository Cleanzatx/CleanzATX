What the Lake Travis page covers

This page gathers the lakeside neighborhoods north of Lakeway that do not belong to any one city. The center of it is the Hudson Bend peninsula, surrounded on three sides by the main basin, with Hudson Bend Rd and Eck Lane running out to the marinas. Travis Landing and the two Hudson Bend Colony plats are the older cabins and ranch homes now being rebuilt, and The Reserve at Hudson Bend is the quiet cul de sac of roughly 40 homes on acre and a half lots. Apache Shores, one of the first developments on the lake, spreads its wooded lots along the Lake Austin side. Canopy at Hudson Bend is the newest gated street on the peninsula.

The gated lakefront enclaves sit along the shore between here and Lakeway. Costa Bella has 62 Mediterranean estates on one to four acres with its own marina, Bella Strada shares that marina next door, and Vineyard Bay holds large custom homes on lots of about an acre and a half three miles north of Lakeway. Lakewind Estates sits above the water with 55 homes on hilltop lots. Villas on Travis, the gated condo community across from Hudson Bend Middle School, and the two Commanders Point communities by Mansfield Dam round out the list. Buffalo Gap, Windmill Bluff Estates, Woods of Lake Travis and Vista Grande are the smaller pockets, each with its own page below.

Streets and landmarks: Hudson Bend Rd, Eck Lane, RM 620, General Williamson Dr.

What lakeside glass deals with

Nothing in our service area is harder on windows than a lakefront lot. Wind off the main basin carries fine spray onto the water facing glass, and when it dries it leaves a white mineral haze that ordinary washing cannot move. Add sprinkler overspray on the other three sides and most waterfront homes have some degree of hard water spotting by the time we first see them. Left for a few summers it etches the surface, so we treat it early, at $10 per pane, before it becomes permanent. Cedar pollen from December to February coats everything out here because the peninsula is thick with juniper, and oak pollen follows in spring.

The view is the point of a Lake Travis home, which means the biggest glass faces the water and usually the sunset. West facing panes show every streak at five in the afternoon, and that is the moment most owners look through them. Pure water is the fix. Our dual RO, four stage system with a DI resin tank gets the water to near 0 TDS, so it dries without spots, film or residue even on a breezy day. Screens on wooded Apache Shores lots fill with webs and leaf litter, and tracks on sliding doors to the deck collect grit, so both are worth adding at $5 each while we are there.

How we work the lake side

From Lakeway the whole area is a short run north on RM 620, so we build lake days that start on Hudson Bend Rd and work back toward the dam or down to Apache Shores. Gated enclaves such as Costa Bella, Vineyard Bay, Lakewind Estates and Canopy need a gate code or our name at the call box; Villas on Travis and the Commanders Point communities work through their associations. Many lakeside lots are steep, with the water side two or three stories above grade. Water fed poles handle that from the ground, so no ladder goes on a sloped deck. Lead time is usually within two weeks, and we quote by text from the square footage.

Homes here range from 1,500 sq ft cabins in Travis Landing to 5,000 sq ft estates at The Reserve at Hudson Bend, so prices range too. A 3,000 sq ft Apache Shores home is about $300 for exterior windows and takes two to three hours; a 4,500 sq ft Reserve home is about $450 and takes most of a morning. Interiors add about 30 minutes per 1,000 sq ft. Lake spray also streaks limestone and stucco, so a house soft wash pairs well with the windows here, from $0.20 per sq ft, and it never involves pressure on stone. Gutters full of juniper litter are the usual fall add on.

Lake Travis questions

Do you still serve Hudson Bend?

Yes. Hudson Bend, Travis Landing, the Colony plats, The Reserve at Hudson Bend and Canopy are all covered by this Lake Travis page and the neighborhood pages below. Only the page name changed.

How much is window cleaning for a lakefront home?

Same as anywhere: $10 per window outside, about $0.10 per sq ft of home, $200 minimum. Hard water removal is extra at $10 per pane, and most lakefront homes need some of it.

Can you remove the white haze on my water side windows?

Usually, yes. Those are mineral deposits from spray and sprinklers, treated at $10 per pane. Glass that has etched for years may not come back fully, and we will say so before we start.

Do you work inside gated communities like Costa Bella?

Yes. Send the gate code or add us to the call box list the day before and we handle the rest. We are insured for $1M, which most gated associations ask about.

How often should lakeside windows be cleaned?

Quarterly keeps spray from setting, and the Quarterly plan is 15% off with hard water removal on up to 5 windows included each visit. Twice a year works for homes set back from the water.

Do you clean Apache Shores and the wooded lots?

Yes. Apache Shores is on our regular lake route. Tree cover there means more webs and leaf litter than average, so we usually suggest adding screens and tracks at $5 each.
