What Davenport Summit homes are like

Davenport Summit sits on Davenport Divide inside Sweetwater, and it is one of the two gated sections of the community. Homes are priced from about $900,000 to $1.5 million, a clear step up from the median across Sweetwater. The Sweetwater HOA governs it, with higher dues for the gated sections. Fewer homes and a gate mean a quiet feel, and owners often like to know exactly who comes through.

Estate style means big stone and stucco elevations, tall entries, and a lot of glass on the back side where covered patios look out over the Hill Country.

What Davenport Summit windows deal with

Height on the divide draws wind, and wind draws dust. Even with a gate, windows here collect dust blown off nearby grading, because Sweetwater is still building out. The dust settles on tall panes and transoms, and dew bonds it to the glass. Sprinklers from large lots hit the lower sections of the wall, leaving hard water spots that can etch with time.

Pollen follows the seasons, cedar from December to February and oak in March and April. West facing back windows in afternoon sun reveal any residue, so pure water matters. The stone is soft washed, never pressure washed.

Davenport Summit questions

How do you handle the gate?

Text us the code or the gate contact with your address. We keep it on the job sheet and confirm the day before.

Why do my tall windows get so dusty?

Lots up on the divide catch wind, and nearby building adds dust. Pure water cleaning rinses it off and leaves no film for new dust to cling to.

Can you reach the top windows safely?

Yes. Our poles reach three stories from the ground, and interior two story windows are done with cotton covered ladders and drop cloths.

Do you hook up to my hose bib?

No. We bring 200 gallons and a pure water system on the skid, so there is no need to hook up to your spigot.

Will you soft wash my stone?

Yes, and stone is only ever soft washed. House soft wash begins at $0.20 per square foot, $400 minimum.
