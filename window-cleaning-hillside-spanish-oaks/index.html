What The Hillside homes are like

The Hillside at Spanish Oaks is a small group of estates inside an already gated golf club community, and you pass through a double gate to reach it. There is not much published about this section beyond larger estate lots and extra privacy. Homes here are custom builds in the $3M to $8M range, which usually means tall glass, wide window walls and deep covered porches facing the Hill Country views the lots were chosen for.

Because the community is small, you will not see many service trucks on the street. Most owners here want the work done quietly, on a set day, by people who show up when they say and leave nothing behind.

What Hillside windows deal with

Big custom glass shows everything. Sprinkler overspray from the large landscaped lots dries into white mineral spots, and in Hill Country water that film can etch into the glass if it sits for seasons. Cedar pollen in winter and live oak pollen in spring coat the outside panes, and oak and juniper cover brings webs and leaf litter into screens and tracks.

Tall stairwell windows and second story glass are common on estates this size. Our water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we can clean the exterior without leaning a ladder on stucco, stone or a finished wall.

How we work The Hillside

We need the gate sorted ahead of time. Send us your gate code or tell us who to call, and we plan around the double gate so the crew is not waiting at the entry. Estate lots mean longer walks between sides of the house, so we load the truck for the full job and stay put until it is done. Stone and stucco elevations can be soft washed on the same trip, and we never pressure wash limestone.

A 6,000 square foot home is about $600 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft, or $10 per window. Exterior time on site runs about 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft, so plan on most of a day for a house that size, with interior by hand on top.

The Hillside questions

Do you handle the double gate at The Hillside?

Yes. Send the gate code or the contact the gate should call, and we schedule around it. A short text the day before keeps the entry smooth, and we confirm our arrival window by text.

How much does a large Hillside home cost to clean?

Outside glass costs $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft. A 6,000 square foot home is about $600. Doing the inside too is $15 per window. The minimum job is $200, and quotes come by text.

Can you clean tall stairwell and second story glass?

Exterior glass up to three stories is cleaned from the ground with water fed poles. Inside, we use ladders with cotton covers and drop cloths on two story interiors so floors and finishes stay protected.

Will you leave spots from the sprinkler water on my glass?

We clean with a 4 stage dual RO system and DI resin, water near 0 TDS, so panes dry without spots or film. Existing hard water spots are removed for $10 per pane.
