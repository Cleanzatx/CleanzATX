The Hills and Flintrock neighborhoods

The Village of The Hills is its own municipality off Lohmans Crossing and RM 620, and this page covers it along with the Flintrock communities that share its club membership. The Hills of Lakeway is the original guard gated section, more than 600 homes on the Nicklaus signature course, built from the 1980s through today. Flintrock Falls is the newer gated golf community on the second Nicklaus course, with custom homes of 3,000 to 6,000 sq ft. Around it sit Flintrock at Hurst Creek, the Estates of Flintrock, Creekside at Flintrock on both the east and west sides, Palomba at Flintrock and the small Escavera enclave that the fairways wrap on every side.

Inside and around the gates are a few attached communities that we clean on the same route: the Villas at Flintrock, the Vistas of Flintrock, and the Crystal Springs and Lakeside and Greens condominiums on The Hills Dr. The Bluffs at Flintrock sits just outside, across from the course and backing to Falconhead West. Destiny Hills, the acreage pocket off TX 71 near Bee Creek with its individually gated driveways, rounds out the group. Every one of these has a page in the grid below. If your home is on either course or behind either gate, you are on a route we run through The Hills most weeks of the year.

Streets and landmarks: The Hills Dr, Flintrock Rd, Lohmans Crossing Rd.

What golf course glass deals with

Golf course irrigation is the first thing we look for in The Hills. Fairway sprinklers run at night and the overspray lands on the back glass of homes along the course, drying into white rings before the sun comes up. After a season or two those rings etch, so we treat them at $10 per pane and flag any windows that are getting close. Lot sprinklers do the same thing on the front and side glass. The village is heavily wooded with live oak and cedar, which means a thick pollen coat from December through April, leaf litter in every track and screen, and spiders working the eaves all summer long.

Most Hills homes are two story stucco or limestone with big rear windows facing the fairway and, in many cases, the western sun. That glass is where streaks show, so we never use soap on it. Our pure water system, a dual RO four stage unit with a DI resin tank, brings the water to near 0 TDS and it dries spotless with no film to attract the next layer of dust. Newer builds at Flintrock Falls and Palomba often come with fine construction dust and hard water from a brand new sprinkler system, and a first thorough cleaning with tracks and screens done sets the baseline for everything after.

How we work The Hills

The Hills and Flintrock Falls both have staffed gates, so we ask you to call in our name the day before or add us to your resident list. Once we are inside we try to stack jobs, because the drive from one fairway street to the next is only a few minutes and that is what keeps the price at $10 per window. Water fed poles reach three stories from the ground, so no ladder leans on stucco, stone or the gutters, and nothing touches your landscaping beyond a hose. Interior glass is done by hand with squeegees over drop cloths. Lead time is usually within two weeks, and we are based ten minutes away in Lakeway.

Homes here run 2,000 to 7,000 sq ft, with 3,500 to 4,500 the most common size, so exterior window cleaning is about $350 to $450 for a typical house and about $600 for a 6,000 sq ft Flintrock Falls custom. Time on site is 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft outside, about 30 inside. Screens run $5 apiece and tracks $5 a window. Golf course lots gather leaf litter in the gutters, so gutter cleaning at $2.25 per linear foot for two stories is a natural pairing in fall, and limestone that has greened up on the shady side of the house gets a soft wash, never pressure.

The Hills questions

How do you get through The Hills gate?

You call in our name to the gatehouse the day before, or add us to your resident guest list. Flintrock Falls works the same way. We confirm the visit by text the evening before.

What does window cleaning cost in The Hills?

About $0.10 per sq ft of home outside, so a 4,000 sq ft home is about $400. That is the same as $10 per window. Inside and out is $15 per window, $200 minimum.

Will the golf course sprinklers spot the windows again?

Yes, over time. A Quarterly or Triannual plan at 15% off keeps ahead of it and includes hard water removal on up to 5 windows each visit, so rings never get a chance to etch.

Do you clean the condos and villas too?

Yes. The Villas at Flintrock, Crystal Springs and Lakeside and Greens are on the same route as the houses. Attached homes still carry the $200 minimum, so neighbors often book together.

Can you reach the high rear windows over the course?

Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we clean the tall fairway facing glass without a ladder on your patio or landscaping, and pure water leaves it spot free.

Do you also wash the house and clean the gutters?

Yes. Limestone and stucco get a soft wash from $0.20 per sq ft, never pressure, and gutters are $2.25 per linear foot on two story homes. Both pair well with a window visit.
