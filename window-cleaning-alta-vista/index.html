What Alta Vista homes are like

Alta Vista sits in the heart of Lakeway with several hundred homes and an active HOA. Prices run from about $500K to $900K, a more approachable range for the area. The neighborhood is not gated. Because it surrounds the town center, residents are a short drive from groceries, restaurants and the lake.

Homes here are a mix of ages and sizes. Some owners have been in place for years and others have moved in recently, so you find both original windows and newer replacements. Lots are mid sized, with trees that have had time to mature.

What Alta Vista windows deal with

Central Lakeway means traffic dust and road grime settling on street facing glass. Trees add pollen, and live oak sheds heavily in March and April. Cedar pollen arrives from December to February. Sprinkler spray on lawns hits windows near the ground and leaves white hard water spots.

Screens collect leaf bits, and tracks collect spider webs and dirt. Neglected tracks make windows harder to open. Our pure water system, near 0 TDS, leaves glass free of soap film, and track cleaning is $5 when you want it done.
