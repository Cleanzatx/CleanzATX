What Vistas at Lakeway homes are like

Toll Brothers and Prominence Homes built these attached homes between 2008 and 2012 and later. They run about 2,400 square feet and sell for $450K to $600K. The homes are off Glen Heather and Origins Lane. There is no gate, and the Vistas at Lakeway condominium association handles shared areas.

The layout means shared walls, common landscaping and fewer exterior surfaces than a detached house. Many owners like the lower upkeep, but windows still need regular care because they face outdoors on only two or three sides.

What Vistas at Lakeway windows deal with

Attached homes often have tall front windows and balconies with glass doors. Dirt and pollen build up on balcony rails and sliding door tracks. Landscaping crews and sprinklers run on shared schedules, so spray from common areas hits the same units again and again, leaving white hard water spots.

Construction in nearby growth areas can send dust onto the glass. Pollen is heavy from December to February for cedar and March to April for live oak. Pure water leaves no residue, and hard water spot removal costs $10 per pane when a spot has dried on.

How we work Vistas at Lakeway

We check with owners about association rules for parking, access and noise before a visit. Without a gate, we simply park where the association allows and walk in. Poles let us reach upper glass from the ground without ladders against shared walls or neighbors' property. For inside work, drop cloths protect floors and we clean by hand. When two neighbors book, we can group the visit.

At 2,400 square feet, a typical Vistas home is about $240 for exterior windows at $10 per window. Plan on about two hours outside. Both sides together run $15 a window. The minimum job is $200, so a smaller unit still meets that minimum.

Vistas at Lakeway questions

Do I need association permission?

Check your condominium association rules on contractors. We usually need only parking and access, and we are insured for $1M if the association asks.

Can you clean balcony glass and railings?

Yes. We clean balcony doors and glass rails, and we reach upper areas with poles when the balcony is out of arm's reach.

Why do my windows spot after sprinklers run?

Sprinkler water carries minerals that dry in white spots on glass. We remove these at $10 per pane, and certain plans include removal on up to 5 windows each visit.

How much does a 2,400 square foot unit cost?

About $240 for the exterior at $0.10 per square foot. Inside and out is $15 per window, and screens are $5 each.

Can neighbors share a visit?

Yes. If two or more neighbors book near each other, we can schedule visits back to back. Each home is quoted and billed separately.
