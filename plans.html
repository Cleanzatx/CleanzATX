Plan questions

What is the Clean Windows Club?

Our recurring window cleaning plans. Choose twice a year, three times a year, quarterly, or every two months. We schedule every visit for you, text a reminder before we arrive, and take 10%, 15% or 20% off every visit, plus the perks that come with your plan.

What comes with each plan?

Twice a year: 10% off every visit and our 7 day touch up guarantee. Triannual, quarterly and every two months: 15%, 15% or 20% off every visit, one free screen cleaning a year, hard water spot removal on up to 5 windows every visit, the 7 day rain guarantee and the 7 day touch up guarantee. Every cleaning, plan or not, includes free sill and track dusting.

What is the Triannual plan?

Three window cleanings a year, about every four months, at 15% off every visit. It comes with every perk the Quarterly plan has: the yearly screen cleaning, hard water removal on up to 5 windows, the 7 day rain guarantee and the 7 day touch up guarantee.

What is the 7 day touch up guarantee?

If you notice a streak or a spot we missed within 7 days of your cleaning, text us and we come back and touch it up for free.

What is the 7 day rain guarantee?

It comes with the Triannual, Quarterly and Every 2 Months plans: if rain affects your results within 7 days of a visit, we come back and reclean for free.

Is there a discount for once a year cleanings?

No. Once a year and one time cleanings are billed at our regular published prices. They still include free sill and track dusting and our 100% satisfaction guarantee.

How do I join?

Pick a plan on this page or choose your frequency in the quote form. We text your price and first open dates, usually within the hour.
