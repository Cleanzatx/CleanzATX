What Overlook homes are like

Overlook is one of the smaller sections of River Place, the master planned community off RM 2222 on the north shore of Lake Austin. The name tells you the point of it. Homes sit on higher ground and are built to look out over the hills and canyon below. Prices run about $900K to $1.5M, so these are well kept family homes rather than sprawling estates. Streets climb and curve the way River Place streets do, with live oak, Ashe juniper and cedar close to the lots. Houses are mostly stucco and stone with a lot of glass on the back, because the back is where the view is.

River Place HOA covers the section, and the community has its own country club and a canyon trail that residents use daily. For us that means tidy yards, steady landscaping crews and driveways that stay open. We park on the street or the driveway, whichever you prefer, and the work stays quiet.

What Overlook windows deal with

View glass is the whole story up here. The big back windows and sliders face open sky and canyon, and when the sun swings around in the afternoon every streak and water spot shows. Sprinklers throw hard water onto the lower panes, and over a season that turns into a white crust that ordinary cleaning will not lift. We use pure water from a dual RO, 4 stage system with a DI resin tank, so the glass dries with no spots and no soap film to catch the light. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so tall back walls on a sloped lot never need a ladder against the house.

Hilltops catch wind, and wind carries cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April. Tree cover sends spider webs and leaf litter into screens and tracks. Screens are $5 each, tracks are $5, and hard water spot removal is $10 per pane when minerals have already set.

How we work Overlook

Overlook is not gated, so access is simple. We come to the door, confirm the plan with you and set up. Most jobs start with the exterior glass from the ground, then the interior by hand with squeegees and drop cloths if you book inside and out. Because the section is small, we usually fold it into other River Place stops on the same route, which keeps scheduling within about two weeks. Gutters and driveways pair well with windows here, since steep drainage sends grit and leaves down the slope. House soft wash starts at $0.20 per sq ft with a $400 minimum, and it brings dull stucco back.

Take a 3,500 sq ft home. Exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft come to about $350, and inside and out is $15 per window. Plan on roughly two and a half to three and a half hours for the outside and about an hour and three quarters for the inside.

My view windows look hazy even after cleaning. Why?

Haze that returns right away is often hard water from sprinklers or lake spray. Pure water stops new spotting, and hard water removal at $10 per pane works on mineral that has already set.
