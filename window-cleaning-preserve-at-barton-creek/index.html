What Preserve homes are like

About 100 homes sit on one to two acre custom lots that back to the Barton Creek preserve, behind a gate and under the Preserve at Barton Creek HOA. Listings show homes of 4,800 to 5,300 square feet, priced around $1.6M to $2.5M. These are large, similar sized houses with plenty of windows on the back, where the views are.

The Austin mailing address can confuse people, since it is just minutes from Bee Cave. We treat it as part of the same route as the other Bee Cave Road neighborhoods.

Streets and landmarks: Preserve Drive.

What Preserve windows deal with

Backing onto protected land means native oak and cedar close to the house. That is good for shade and bad for glass: pollen in winter and spring, spider webs across upper frames, and leaf litter packed into screens and tracks. Any place with that much undeveloped cover also gets steady dust on the back windows.

Large homes often have big rear window walls with sprinklers throwing water onto the lower glass. The white spots that result are hard water minerals, and they are best removed before they set. We use water near 0 TDS so panes finish clean.

How we work The Preserve

We confirm the gate with you before the day, and the community is close to the junction where our Lakeway route meets Bee Cave Road, so travel is short. Owners often add the screens and tracks to a window visit, since the tree cover loads them up. Soft washing the stucco and stone is a natural pairing, and we never pressure wash limestone.

Take a 5,000 square foot house: about $500 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft. Count on roughly four hours for the exterior at 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft, with interior glass cleaned by hand in about two and a half hours more.

The Preserve at Barton Creek questions

Is The Preserve at Barton Creek in Austin or Bee Cave?

The mailing city is Austin 78735, but it is right by Bee Cave Road and TX 71. We serve it from Lakeway as part of the same corridor, and the quote does not change because of the mailing address.

How much does a 5,000 square foot home cost?

Outside glass comes to about $500 at $0.10 per sq ft, or $10 per window. Add the inside for $15 per window. Screens are $5 and the minimum job is $200.

Do trees against the back of my house change anything?

They add work to screens and tracks. We clean those for $5 each, and for trees pressing close to glass we brush off webs and debris before the water goes on.

How do you deal with the community gate?

Send the gate code or the contact that will let us in. We plan arrival around it and confirm by text. If the HOA has contractor rules, share them with the quote request.

Can you clean stucco and stone at the same visit?

Yes. A soft wash for the exterior walls begins at $0.20 per sq ft, $400 minimum. It is gentle enough for stucco and limestone, which we never pressure wash.
