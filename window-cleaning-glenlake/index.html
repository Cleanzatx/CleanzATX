What Glenlake homes are like

Glenlake is a wooded neighborhood where lots run from 1.5 to 5.75 acres. Most homes date from the 1980s and 1990s. They cover a huge range, from about 1,500 square feet to more than 8,000, and prices fall between about $1.35 million and $1.9 million. Some have views toward Lake Austin. The Glenlake HOA oversees the community and River Pointe is part of it.

Every house is different, so size matters more than street. A small older house and a very large one can sit a few lots apart.

What Glenlake windows deal with

Time takes a toll. Houses from the 1980s and 1990s have seen many years of sprinkler spray, and hard water marks on glass can build into etching. Frames and tracks collect grit, and old screens may have faded or loosened.

Tree cover and acreage mean webs, leaf litter and pollen. Cedar pollen in winter and live oak pollen in spring land thickly, and views toward the lake make any film on view glass easy to see.

How we work Glenlake

Because home sizes vary so much, we price from your photos and window count rather than lot size. A small house needs the $200 minimum, while a large one takes a full day. Poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so view glass is no problem. Acreage means long drives, so we plan parking near the house and bring 200 gallons of water. Many owners add a roof soft wash or driveway cleaning for homes of this age.

For a 3,000 square foot home, exterior windows run about $300 and take two to three hours. For an 8,000 square foot house the same work is about $800 and a long day, so tell us the size when you text.

Glenlake questions

Does the size of the house really change the price?

Yes. Exterior windows are about $0.10 per square foot, so a 1,500 square foot house is about $200 and an 8,000 square foot house is about $800. Minimum job is $200.

Is River Pointe included?

Yes. River Pointe is part of Glenlake, and we serve it the same way. Tell us which section you are in when you text.

Can you clean old tracks and screens?

Yes. Tracks are $5 each and screens are $5 each, with $10 for full screens and $15 for solar screens. Older tracks can hold years of grit, so we brush them out by hand.

How long does a large Glenlake home take?

Exterior glass runs about 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 square feet and interior about 30 minutes per 1,000 square feet. An 8,000 square foot home is a full day.

Do you offer plans for big homes?

Yes. The Clean Windows Club saves 10% to 20% depending on how often you choose. Quarterly gives 15% off and includes hard water removal on up to 5 windows each visit.
