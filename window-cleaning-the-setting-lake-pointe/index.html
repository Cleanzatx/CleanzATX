What The Setting homes are like

This is where Lake Pointe began. The Setting holds roughly 150 homes built in the 1990s, making it the oldest part of the neighborhood, with prices from about $500,000 to $800,000. It belongs to the Lake Pointe HOA and is not gated. The trees here have had thirty years to grow, so streets feel shaded and settled compared to the newer sections nearby.

Original homes from this era often have their first generation windows, with older frames and tracks. They clean up well, but they reward a careful hand around the seals and screens.

What The Setting windows deal with

Age matters for glass. A window that has taken three decades of sprinkler water can carry layers of hard water film that a standard wash will not touch. Our hard water spot removal, at $10 per pane, handles it, and the results show best on windows that have not been treated before. Mature trees add spider webs, acorns and leaf litter in the tracks, and cedar pollen from December to February coats everything.

Exteriors are often stucco or stone with some wood trim. We soft wash those surfaces with the 12V system, because pressure does harm to limestone and aging trim alike. Pure water poles take care of the glass.

How we work The Setting

There is no gate, so we pull in and begin. With older frames, we pay attention to screens and tracks, which get brittle and gather debris. We tell you if a screen is past saving rather than risk damaging it. Many owners schedule a first deep clean with hard water removal, then move to a Twice a Year plan to keep it up, saving 10%.

As an example, a 2,400 square foot home runs about $240 for exterior windows and takes roughly one and three quarter to two and a half hours. Interior at $15 a window brings the total to about $360.

The Setting at Lake Pointe questions

Can old hard water stains come off?

Often, yes. We treat spots at $10 per pane and tell you before we start if the glass looks etched. Etching is damage in the glass itself, and cleaning cannot reverse it.

My windows are original. Are they safe to clean?

Yes. We use pure water on the glass and hand work on the interior. If a frame or screen looks fragile, we point it out first.

What should I do first, deep clean or a plan?

A deep clean with hard water removal comes first. After that, Twice a Year saves 10% and keeps things fresh.

Do you service other Lake Pointe sections?

Yes, including The Landing, The Estates, Vista Pointe and the gated Pointe. We schedule by section to keep routes efficient.

How much is a typical house here?

Exterior windows are about $0.10 per square foot with a $200 minimum, so a 2,400 square foot home is around $240. A house soft wash starts at $0.20 per square foot.
