What Creekside at Flintrock West windows deal with

Shaded glass collects different grime than sunny glass. Oak pollen sticks to damp panes in March and April, cedar pollen follows from December to February, and spider webs build in the eaves and window frames all year. Leaves and seed pods settle into screens and tracks, which can leave a dark line along the bottom edge of the glass. Irrigation is the other regular issue, because sprinklers throw hard water onto lower panes and leave white spots.

Our dual RO, 4 stage pure water system, with a DI resin tank, brings water down to near 0 TDS. Glass dries without spots or soap film, and the frame stays clean. Screens are $5 each, tracks are $5, and hard water spot removal in Creekside at Flintrock West is $10 per pane when deposits need more than a rinse.
