What Tuscan Village homes are like

Legend Communities built Tuscan Village for adults 55 and older. It holds several hundred condos and villas, from about 972 to 3,200 square feet, priced between $300K and $700K. Some sections are gated, and Tuscan Village HOA manages shared areas. It sits near Lakeway Regional, which makes it convenient for daily needs.

Residents here often choose a lock and leave lifestyle. They travel, visit family and spend less time on exterior chores. Window cleaning fits that pattern as a service that happens on a schedule and needs little from the owner.

What Tuscan Village windows deal with

Villas often have patio doors, covered porches and tall front windows. Porch glass collects dust and spider webs quickly, since it sits under a roof and does not get rinsed by rain. Cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April coat everything outdoors.

Sprinkler spray from shared landscaping hits the first row of windows and doors. Mineral spots dry white in the sun. Our water runs near 0 TDS, which leaves a clean finish without soap film, and removal of existing spots costs $10 per pane. Tracks and screens benefit from care at least once a year.

How we work Tuscan Village

For gated sections, we arrange access with you or the HOA in advance. We follow parking and quiet hours and keep the visit short. Poles reach upper glass from the ground, so we do not set ladders on shared walkways. Inside work is done by hand with drop cloths, and we are careful in homes where owners prefer to be present. A recurring plan means the same crew returns on a predictable rhythm.
