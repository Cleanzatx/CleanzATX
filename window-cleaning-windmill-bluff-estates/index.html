What Windmill Bluff Estates homes are like

This is a small subdivision with larger and newer homes than much of the surrounding 78734 area. A recent example runs about 3,755 sq ft and about $1.25M. It is not gated and the records show no HOA, so each owner decides when and how to care for the exterior.

Bluff sites tend to be open to the wind and the sky. That gives the homes light and long sightlines, and it also puts more of the house in direct sun and weather than a house tucked into trees would be.

Streets and landmarks: Cloudy Ridge Road.

What Windmill Bluff Estates windows deal with

Open lots collect dust. Wind off the bluff moves grit and pollen across the glass, and the sun dries any water spot right onto it. Sprinklers watering a big lawn leave mineral rings low on the glass. Where water from sprinklers or lake air has repeatedly dried, the spots can etch the surface over time.

Newer homes carry more glass, with taller panes and bigger doors. Upper windows are the difficult ones to do by hand. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we do them without ladders on a sloped lot.

How we work Windmill Bluff Estates

Without a gate or HOA, you can simply text us for a time. We drive up Cloudy Ridge Road, set the truck near the house and work around it one side at a time. Because the lots slope, we like to get a photo of the drive and any retaining walls before we come. Paving, patios and stone steps can be cleaned later with the surface cleaner or by hand.

A 3,755 sq ft home is about $375 for the outside of the windows at $0.10 per sq ft. We plan roughly three to three and a half hours on site, and about two more for the inside.

Windmill Bluff Estates questions

Do you work on sloped lots?

Yes. We clean exterior glass from the ground with water fed poles, so we are not leaning ladders on uneven ground. A photo of the drive helps us plan where the truck goes.

Why does my glass look dull even after a wash?

Dullness can come from a film left by soap or hard water, or from mineral spots that have started to etch. Pure water avoids leaving a film, and we remove spots at $10 per pane where possible.

How much for the outside of a 3,755 sq ft house?

About $375 at $0.10 per sq ft. If you count windows instead, it is $10 per window. The inside adds to that, and inside and out is $15 per window.

Can you clean the patio and driveway as well?

Yes. Concrete is $0.18 per sq ft with a $250 minimum, and pavers are $0.25. Patios and pool decks are cleaned by hand so they do not get soaked.

What if I need a touch up?

Send a photo by text inside 7 days. When we are in the area, we come back and fix it at no charge.
