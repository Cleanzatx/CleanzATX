What Barton Creek West homes are like

Several hundred homes make up the neighborhood, with two pools, tennis courts and a 400 acre private greenbelt. Barton Creek Elementary and West Ridge Middle School are on site, and the Barton Creek West HOA runs the community. Prices run about $1M to $2.5M, and the lots are large and heavily wooded.

It is mostly not gated, although listings mention some gated lots. That makes scheduling easy for most homes, with a gate code only for the few that need one.

What Barton Creek West windows deal with

Next to a greenbelt, tree cover is the whole story. Cedar and live oak pollen come in waves from December to April, spider webs spread across upper frames, and leaf litter packs the screens and tracks. Shaded walls can grow a light green film on lower panes after a wet spring.

We pay extra attention to tracks and screens here, because a clean pane in a dirty track still looks tired. Screens are $5 each, and tracks are $5, and many homeowners add both to the first visit to reset everything.

How we work Barton Creek West

We schedule by street so a few neighbors can share a day. If your lot is steep, we tell you in the quote and set up the water fed poles from the safest ground we can find. The steady school drop off traffic around the community is something we work around when picking arrival times. Roof soft wash is worth asking about if you have leaves sitting on the shingles.

At $0.10 per sq ft, exterior windows on a 3,500 square foot house are about $350. Exterior time on site is around two and a half to three and a half hours, with interior at about 30 minutes per 1,000 sq ft.

Barton Creek West questions

Is Barton Creek West gated?

Mostly not, though a few lots are gated. If yours is one, send the code with your quote request and we plan around it. Everywhere else we just show up in the window we text you.

What does a typical home cost?

Outside glass is $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft, which puts a 3,500 square foot house at about $350 here. Doing both sides is $15 per window. Minimum job is $200.

Why are my screens so dirty near the greenbelt?

Trees drop pollen, leaves and webs that collect in screens and tracks. We clean screens for $5 and tracks for $5, and a yearly free screen cleaning comes with higher plans.

Can you clean glass on a steep wooded lot?

Usually yes. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we often work from the uphill side. We flag unusual access in the quote before booking.

Do you offer recurring service here?

Yes. Triannual at 15% off suits most wooded homes, with Quarterly at 15% off and Every 2 Months at 20% off for heavy pollen and webs. All include the 7 day rain guarantee.
