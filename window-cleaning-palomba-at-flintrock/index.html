What Palomba at Flintrock homes are like

Palomba is a small gated section inside Flintrock, and the homes are mostly from the 2010s. They run from about 2,605 to 4,144 square feet, and prices sit between roughly $775K and $1.25M. That makes this one of the more approachable corners of the Flintrock area, with a mix of one and two story plans.

The Flintrock HOA looks after the section. Homes from this period usually have wide casement and picture windows, big sliders and glass doors off the patio. By now the lots are mature enough that trees have filled in and shade the yard in summer. A house that is ten years old has also gone through ten rounds of sprinkler water on the lower glass.

What Palomba windows deal with

The main culprit at this age is hard water. A decade of irrigation overspray leaves a visible white crust on the lower panes and on the sills, and it can start to etch. Regular cleaning prevents that, but if it is already set, hard water spot removal at $10 per pane often brings it back. Our dual RO, 4 stage pure water system with a DI resin tank gives water near 0 TDS, so after cleaning the glass dries with no spots and no soap film.

Pollen season runs hard here. Cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April coat everything and stick to screens. Tracks collect grit and webs. We clean screens at $5 each, full screens at $10, and tracks at $5, so a full reset of the windows is easy to price.
