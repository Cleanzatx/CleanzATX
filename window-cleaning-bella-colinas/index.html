What Bella Colinas homes are like

Bella Colinas is a 2010s neighborhood of roughly 300 homes in Bee Cave, with houses from about 1,900 to 6,000 square feet and prices from the high $500,000s to $1.1 million. Families come for the Bee Cave schools, and the Bella Colinas HOA keeps streets and common areas in order. There is no gate. It is a busy family neighborhood, with kids, dogs and basketball goals in driveways.

Houses are stucco and stone, with two story plans that put windows high above the front door and over the garage.

What Bella Colinas windows deal with

Family life leaves marks on glass inside, fingerprints and nose prints at toddler height, and those are cleaned by hand with squeegees. Outside, sprinklers spot the ground floor windows, Bee Cave construction dust drifts in, and pollen arrives twice, cedar from December to February and oak in March and April. Trees planted along the street are growing up and bringing webs.

Second story glass is where most owners give up, since ladders on stucco are risky. Our poles reach three stories from the ground, so the upper windows get the same pure water rinse as the lower ones. Stucco and stone walls are soft washed, never pressure washed.
