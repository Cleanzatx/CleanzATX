What Travis Settlement homes are like

Travis Settlement is built on two acre lots, so homes sit far apart and many have long drives and wooded back yards. It has several hundred homes, priced from about $700K to $1.5M, and a Travis Settlement HOA that charges $220 to $260 a year. There is no gate. The HOA park and private boat ramp give residents lake access, which is a large part of the appeal. Vintage Estate Homes specs are common, meaning many houses share a traditional Hill Country look with stone or brick fronts, metal roofs and covered porches.

Windows are often wide and numerous, since porches and living rooms face the trees.

What Travis Settlement windows deal with

Two acre lots mean a lot of cedar and live oak around the house. Pollen from cedar in the winter and live oak in spring dust the glass, and falling leaves fill screens and tracks. Spiders move into eaves and window frames under tree cover. With lake access nearby, damp air and lake spray bring mineral spots, and irrigation over large lawns adds more hard water residue to lower windows. The tree shade also lets algae grow on lower sills, and afternoon sun shows streaks on clean glass.

Metal roofs and porch covers also drip debris onto the glass below when it rains.
