What Reserve at Lake Travis homes are like

The Reserve is a gated resort style community of 310 acres, with roughly 400 lots. Homes have three to six bedrooms on lots up to an acre, priced from about $1M to $5M. Amenities include a marina, a yacht club, a swim park with a lazy river, a restaurant and stables, and a Reserve at Lake Travis HOA runs the grounds. Houses lean large and detailed, with stone, stucco and metal roofs, wide patios and long glass walls facing the water or the hills. This is the far northwest edge of the area we serve, so a visit takes planning.

A community like this has a lot of glass, and much of it faces the lake.

What Reserve at Lake Travis windows deal with

Waterfront glass has the hardest job. Spray and humidity from the lake leave a mineral haze, and west facing windows catch low sun that reveals every streak. Irrigation over large lawns and the resort landscaping leaves hard water scale on lower panes that can etch if it is left for years. Cedar pollen in winter and live oak pollen in spring cover surfaces, and the stables and open land nearby add dust. Decks and patios under the windows get foot traffic that kicks dust up on the glass.

Tall walls of glass also gather webs and insects around the frames, mainly near trees and lights.

How we work Reserve at Lake Travis

The gate is the first step. Send us the code or the contact who can open it, and we confirm arrival by text. Because it is a drive from Bee Cave, we group stops, so neighbors who book the same day make the trip more efficient. Our skid carries 200 gallons for the pure water system. Inside, we hand squeegee with drop cloths, and cotton covered ladders for two story rooms. Patios and pool decks are cleaned by hand, and stucco and limestone are soft washed only.
