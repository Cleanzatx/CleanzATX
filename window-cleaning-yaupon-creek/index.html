What Yaupon Creek homes are like

The neighborhood is small, with homes priced from $600K to $1M. It has no gate and no HOA on record. Its main draw is location, since it sits close to the center of Lakeway and the shops and services around it. Residents can reach the lake, the grocery store and the schools within a few minutes.

A small subdivision like this tends to have owners who know each other. When one house gets clean windows, the neighbors notice, and a few often call within a week or two.

What Yaupon Creek windows deal with

Because it sits close to the town center, glass here picks up road dust and the fine grit that comes off busy streets. Trees around the lots add pollen, cedar from December to February and live oak in March and April. Leaf bits land in tracks, and spiders settle in window corners.

Irrigation is the quiet culprit. Sprinklers aimed at beds can hit the first row of windows twice a week, and minerals dry into white dots. The spots look like dirt but do not wipe away. Our water runs near 0 TDS, so rinsing leaves nothing behind, and removal of old spots is $10 per pane.

How we work Yaupon Creek

Without a gate, we simply arrive and set up. Water fed poles reach the upper glass from the ground, up to three stories. We hand clean inside windows with squeegees and keep drop cloths under every work area. Since the neighborhood is close to the center of Lakeway, we can often fit it between larger jobs. Many owners add gutters and screens to the visit, or choose a house soft wash if the shade has grown algae.

We have no published size band for this neighborhood, so we quote by the home. A 3,000 square foot house comes to about $300 for exterior windows. Allow two to three hours outside, and a bit under two hours more for interior cleaning.

Yaupon Creek questions

How do I know what my home will cost?

Send your address and a rough square footage or window count. Exterior windows are $10 each, or about $0.10 per square foot, and the minimum job is $200.

Is my glass etched or just dirty?

Often it is just mineral film from sprinklers. Send a photo and we tell you honestly. Removal is $10 per pane if the spots have not bitten into the glass.

Do you service gutters and screens here too?

Yes. Screens are $5 each and gutters are $1.50, $2.25 or $3.25 per linear foot for one, two or three stories.

Can neighbors share a service day?

Yes. If several households on your street book close together, we schedule them back to back. Each home is quoted on its own.

What if I am unhappy with the result?

Tell us. We have a 100% satisfaction guarantee, and a 7 day touch up that brings us back at no charge when we are in the area.
