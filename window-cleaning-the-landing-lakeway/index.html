What The Landing at Lakeway homes are like

The Landing is a small private enclave of newly built villas priced from $600K to $900K. The gate keeps traffic limited, and the villas are close to the Galleria's shops and restaurants. No HOA is on record. Details on this community are limited, so we ask owners for the specifics when they text.

Newer homes arrive with all their windows in good shape, but new builds carry their own mess. Fresh glass often has stickers, paint flecks and stucco splatter left from the build.

What The Landing at Lakeway windows deal with

Newly finished homes sit in fresh landscaping, and bare soil kicks dust onto the glass. Sod and new beds get daily watering, which sprays lower windows. Mineral spots form fast when water dries on warm glass. Pollen is the same as everywhere, cedar from December to February and live oak from March to April.

New glass is easier to clean than old glass, and a good start protects it. Spots caught in the first year do not etch. Pure water at near 0 TDS rinses clean without film. Light paint flecks and splatter can often be removed with care. Removal of mineral spots is $10 per pane.

How we work The Landing at Lakeway

Gate access comes first. Share the code or a contact, and we arrange the visit around it. We use poles from the ground, up to three stories, and avoid ladders on new paint and stucco. Inside windows are done by hand with squeegees, and drop cloths protect new floors. A first cleaning after landscaping settles is best, and a plan keeps dust and spray from building up.

The Landing at Lakeway questions

When should I first clean a new villa?

Wait until sod, beds and nearby building work are mostly done. That keeps the glass clear longer. If you need it for a move in, we can clean and then touch up later.

Can you remove stickers and paint flecks?

Often yes, with care and pure water. Heavy cement splatter or etched glass is different, and we tell you what we see before starting.

How do I give you gate access?

Text the gate code or the contact name, and we plan the visit around it.

What does a villa cost?

Outside glass is $10 a window, or about $0.10 per square foot of home. Villas often land between $200 and $300, with a $200 minimum.

Do you have a plan for new homeowners?

Yes. Twice a Year takes 10% off. Triannual takes 15% and includes a yearly free screen cleaning and a 7 day rain guarantee.
