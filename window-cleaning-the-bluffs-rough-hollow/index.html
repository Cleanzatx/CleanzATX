What The Bluffs is like

The Bluffs is a section of high lots above the water in Rough Hollow. Prices run from $1M to $2M. The draw is seeing the Hill Country and the lake together, so rear windows tend to be large and decks tend to be open to the view. The section is not gated.

Slopes are part of the character. Some lots step down toward the back, and where that happens the lower level adds glass the front never shows.

What Bluffs windows deal with

Wind is the signature issue. Open exposure means dust and pollen blow straight onto the glass, and in cedar season the panes go yellow quickly. View windows are often oversized, so a single streak is easy to see in the afternoon light.

The slope makes ladder work awkward, and that is part of why water fed poles suit this section. On a sloped lot the poles reach three stories from the lowest level of ground. Spiders also love deck railings and eaves, so webs are a regular find.

How we work The Bluffs

We come in from the street and reach the back by the side yard, so we stay off your lawn when we can. A few homes have walkout levels, and we clean those windows too. House soft wash on stucco pairs well with window cleaning, and decks and patios are cleaned by hand.

A 4,000 sq ft Bluffs home is about $400 for exterior windows, taking roughly three hours. Add inside at $15 a window overall. Text us for a quote, and expect an answer inside the hour during business hours.

The Bluffs questions

Why does my back glass get dirty faster than the front?

The back faces open air on high ground. Wind pushes dust and pollen straight onto it, and spider webs collect along the deck.

Can you clean the walkout level windows?

Yes. The poles reach from wherever the ground is, so lower level glass is part of the same visit.

Do you pressure wash stone on the property?

No. Limestone is never pressure washed. We soft wash it using our 12V system, then rinse gently.

What is the minimum price?

Our minimum job is $200. Exterior windows are $10 each, and most Bluffs homes come in around $350 to $450.

Do you clean inside windows too?

Yes. Cleaning both sides is $15 a window. We hand squeegee the interior over drop cloths and use cotton covered ladders in two story rooms.

Will wind spoil the clean right away?

Wind brings dust, but pure water leaves no soap film, so dirt does not stick as fast. The 7 day touch up covers any problem in the first week.
