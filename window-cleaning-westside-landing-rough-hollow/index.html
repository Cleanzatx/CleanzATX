What Westside Landing is like

Westside Landing sits on the western edge of Rough Hollow. It began with 41 homes and mixes townhome style units with some single family homes. Prices run around $500K to $900K. No gate stands between you and the street, and the Rough Hollow HOA applies.

Units are lock and leave, so many owners travel. Walls are shared or close, windows are fewer, and the front and back elevations carry most of the glass. With the original 41 homes and the neighboring units, it is one of the more compact parts of the community.

What Westside Landing windows deal with

The western edge gets more afternoon sun on the back of each unit. Glass that faces west shows streaks, and fingerprints on sliders are very visible. Dust and pollen from the road side settle on front windows.

Smaller yards mean sprinkler heads sit near the glass, so hard water spots are common on lower panes. Tight patios collect spider webs. We clean sliders and tracks together, since patio doors do most of the work here. A lot of the dirt is just daily life: doors opened and closed, dogs at the sliders, kids at the glass.

How we work Westside Landing

Parking can be tight, so we plan a spot ahead and keep hoses neat. Poles cover two story units from the ground. Window cleaning is the main job, and a patio hand clean pairs well with it.

Many units come near the $200 minimum job. A 1,800 sq ft unit is $200, and a 2,200 sq ft unit is about $220. Interior adds about 30 minutes per 1,000 sq ft. Triannual saves 15% and keeps recurring visits automatic. Our minimum job keeps the price clear for these smaller homes.

Westside Landing questions

Is my unit small enough to hit the minimum?

Probably, if it is under about 2,000 sq ft. The minimum job is $200, and we confirm after you text details.

Do you need access to the inside?

Only for interior glass. Exterior work can go ahead without anyone home.

Can you clean patio doors and tracks?

Yes. Tracks are $5 each, and sliders are cleaned as part of the glass.

Do you wash the front of the building?

We soft wash siding and stucco where the HOA and owner allow, starting at $0.20 per sq ft with a $400 minimum.

Do you offer plans for smaller units?

Yes. The same plans apply, from Twice a Year at 10% off up to Every 2 Months at 20% off. Most owners pick Triannual.

Can you do two units next to each other?

Yes. Neighbors can book the same morning, and each unit is priced on its own.
