Homes in Montview Acres sit on big lots rather than the tight lots of newer subdivisions. A typical sale price is around $1.65 million, and the neighborhood is small. There is no gate and no HOA listed. The breathing room between houses gives each one a private feel.

What Montview Acres windows deal with

On acreage there is more nature near the house. Trees drop leaf litter into screens and tracks, webs gather in corners, and cedar and live oak pollen stick to the glass in season. Dust from unpaved edges and nearby construction settles on sills.

Irrigation on larger lawns is a steady source of hard water. Sprinkler heads that reach windows leave white mineral marks, and over a few seasons the marks etch. Lake air nearby adds to it.
