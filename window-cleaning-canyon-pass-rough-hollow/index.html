What Canyon Pass is like

Canyon Pass is a larger section at the entry to Rough Hollow, with greenbelt homesites. It has the broadest range of home sizes in the community, and prices from the high $300Ks in 2018 up to about $900K. It is not gated. It is also known for high turnover, so new owners arrive often.

That mix means every job differs. A smaller home may be near the $200 minimum, while a bigger family house can pass $400. Greenbelt lots back onto open land, giving a different view than the lake sections.

What Canyon Pass windows deal with

A greenbelt behind the house brings pollen, seeds, insects and spiders. Cobwebs hang from eaves and sliders, and screens clog with seed fluff. Backing onto open land also brings dust.

Many Canyon Pass homes are a few years old, and new owners often want the first clean after move in. Stickers, paint specks and stucco splatter can still sit on the glass. We remove them carefully and finish with pure water. Backing onto a greenbelt is a plus for views, though the glass pays for it.

How we work Canyon Pass

Since Canyon Pass is the entry to Rough Hollow, it is the easiest place to start a route, and we often use it as the first stop of a morning that continues up into the hilltop sections. Access is simple, and the Rough Hollow HOA is the only association.

A smaller home of 1,800 sq ft hits the $200 minimum. A 2,800 sq ft home is about $280 outside, taking around two hours, and the inside adds about 90 minutes. Screens are $5 each. For new owners, a first visit and then a Twice a Year plan saves 10%. We can usually fit a new job in within two weeks.
