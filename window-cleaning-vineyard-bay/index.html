What Vineyard Bay homes are like

The community is small and gated, and the lots are big, around 1.5 acres each. Homes are custom builds with gourmet kitchens and large entertaining spaces, and prices range from $1.5M to $4M. It sits about three miles north of Lakeway, which puts it on the quieter side of the lake, away from the busier stretches of RR 620.

The Vineyard Bay HOA looks after the community, and each home sits well back from the next. Several face the water, so glass runs from kitchen walls to upstairs bedroom windows with a lake view.

What Vineyard Bay windows deal with

Waterfront lots take the most weather. Lake mist carries minerals, and when it dries on glass it leaves white spots. Wide lake view windows also catch low afternoon sun, which exposes every streak and bit of film left by a regular wash. That is the reason we use pure water: nothing dries onto the glass.

Large lots usually have lots of irrigation, and sprinkler overspray on lower panes adds more mineral spotting. Cedar and live oak pollen fall on the screens and tracks each season. Spot removal is $10 per pane, and screen cleaning is $5 each.

How we work Vineyard Bay

The gate comes first. Send us the code or the contact name when you book. With lots this big, we plan the walk from truck to every side of the house and any detached buildings, and we usually set aside a half day for a larger home. Water fed poles reach three stories, which helps with tall two story great rooms and upper balconies. Windows and a house soft wash often go together here, particularly on the lake side of the house where moisture sits longest.

A 4,500 sq ft custom home comes to about $450 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft. Plan on roughly three and a half to four and a half hours on site for the outside.

Vineyard Bay questions

Do you work inside gated communities like Vineyard Bay?

Yes. We just need the gate code or a contact who can let us in on the day of the job. Tell us when you text, and we will plan the whole day around that access.

Why do my lake facing windows look streaky in the afternoon?

Low sun makes any film or drying mark stand out. Pure water leaves no soap film and no minerals on the glass, so there is much less to catch the light. That is the main reason our work looks clearer on these windows.

How long will a large home take?

Exterior takes about 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft, and interior about 30 minutes per 1,000 sq ft. A big custom house can take a half day, and we will tell you when we quote.

Can you clean the dock or boathouse glass?

If the glass is part of your property and reachable with our poles from the ground or the dock, we can quote it. Text us a photo and we will tell you what makes sense.
