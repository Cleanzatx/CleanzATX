What Destiny Hills windows deal with

Tree cover is the defining condition. Oaks and cedars shade the lots, and they drop pollen, leaves and seeds onto the glass. Cedar pollen runs from December to February and live oak pollen in March and April. Spiders build in the eaves and window corners, and webs gather in the tracks. Dust also drifts in from long gravel or dirt drives, and rain splashes it up onto lower panes.

Wells and hoses give hard water on acreage, and lawn sprinklers leave white spots. Our dual RO, 4 stage pure water system with a DI resin tank gets water near 0 TDS, which lets Destiny Hills glass dry with no spots and no soap film. Screens are $5 each, tracks are $5, and hard water spot removal in Destiny Hills is $10 per pane.
