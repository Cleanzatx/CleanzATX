What The Summit is like

The Summit is the high point of Rough Hollow, and the homes here start at about $1M. It is a small section, it is not gated, and it falls under the Rough Hollow HOA. Because of the height, it has the widest views in the community.

Expect large windows facing outward and, in some cases, glass on every side of the house. Rooflines are higher and more exposed than in lower sections, which means more wind and more sun on the glass. Few trees reach this high, so there is little shade to soften the sun.

What Summit windows deal with

Wind is steady at the top of the ridge. It drives pollen into window seals and dust onto the glass, and it does so more quickly than in sheltered sections. After a windy week, even a freshly cleaned pane looks tired.

Rain is driven by the wind at this height, which can leave mud splatter along the lower panes and runoff marks below the sills. We use pure water so the glass dries clean, and the 7 day touch up covers any rain that falls soon after.

How we work The Summit

Our crew plans around the wind. We start on the side that is most sheltered, keep the poles steady and finish where the gusts are strongest when they are calm. The Summit pairs well with Vista Ridge Estates and The Overlook for scheduling.

A 4,000 sq ft home is about $400 for exterior windows, which takes around three hours outside. Interior work runs at roughly 30 minutes a thousand square feet, so add two hours. If you want to spread the cost across the year, the Twice a Year plan saves 10%.

The Summit questions

Is it harder to clean windows at the highest point?

Wind is the main thing. We adjust the order and wait for calmer moments, but the job is no harder for us.

Do you wash the siding too?

Yes. House soft wash starts at $0.20 per sq ft with a $400 minimum, and it works well on stucco.

What if it rains after the visit?

Text a photo within 7 days. We return free when we are in the area, and plan members have the 7 day rain guarantee.

Is a plan worth it for an exposed home?

Often. Quarterly takes 15% off and lets us catch dust and pollen before it builds up.

Do you work in the cedar season?

Yes. Cedar pollen runs December to February, and we clean through it. The glass will gather new pollen quickly, so some owners book a visit just after the peak passes.

Can you clean the high gable windows?

Yes, up to three stories from the ground with water fed poles. Taller gables beyond that reach are looked at individually, and we say so up front.
