What Falconhead West homes are like

This is the newer side of Falconhead. The plats read Falconhead West, Phase 1, Section 1 and on, and the first phase alone holds more than 165 homes, with several hundred across the whole area. Houses were built through the 2000s and into the 2010s, from about 1,900 to 4,900 square feet. The area is not gated, and it has its own Falconhead West HOA. Bee Cave Elementary is part of the picture for many families, so weekday mornings are busy and we plan around school traffic.

Exteriors are mostly stucco with limestone accents, the Hill Country look, and windows are the modern kind with big picture panes in the living room and across the back. Prices run from the mid $500s into the $1.4 millions, which means a lot of owners want a careful hand on trim and sills.

What Falconhead West windows deal with

Being close to Highway 71 matters. Traffic, road dust and the steady construction around Bee Cave settle on glass and sills, and a wet morning turns that dust into a gray film. Some lots here still have young landscaping, which means sprinklers hit the lower panes and leave hard water spots. Where trees have grown in, spider webs collect in corners and screens catch leaf litter.

Pollen is the seasonal piece. Cedar comes first, December to February, then live oak in March and April, and both coat window frames and stucco. We run the pure water pole system for the exterior and hand wash the interior with squeegees. Any limestone gets a soft wash, never a pressure washer.
