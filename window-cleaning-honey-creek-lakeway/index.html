What Honey Creek homes are like

The homes here are tightly grouped in size, between 1,777 and 1,912 square feet, and are priced from $600K to $750K. Almost all are three bedrooms. There is no gate and no HOA. With so little variation, neighbors often compare notes about upkeep.

A house of about 1,800 square feet has around 18 windows, using our average of 10 windows per 1,000 square feet. That makes the job easy to estimate and plan, and most Honey Creek owners get a quote in one text.

What Honey Creek windows deal with

Typical issues apply. Pollen from cedar in winter and live oak in spring covers the glass. Trees at the front and rear drop leaves into screens. Mineral spots from sprinklers collect on the lower row of panes.

Homes of similar age weather the same way, so neighbors often share the same hard water pattern. If one house has etched lower panes, the one next door probably does too. Cleaning on a regular schedule keeps spots from setting. Removal for existing spots is $10 per pane.
