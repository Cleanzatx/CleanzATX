What Long Canyon homes are like

Long Canyon covers roughly 300 homes on lots of about 1 to 5.7 acres. Most were built between 1983 and 2007 as custom homes, so no two look alike. Sizes run from about 2,300 up to 6,500 sq ft, and prices sit around $900K to $1.8M. The setting is canyon and Hill Country, with views toward Lake Travis country from the higher lots. Roofs are tile, metal or composition, walls are stone, stucco or siding, and many homes have a wall of glass facing the canyon. Long Canyon HOA oversees the area, and it is not gated.

You get privacy and big skies here, and homes are far enough apart that the road in is a drive rather than a walk.

Streets we work: RM 2222.

What Long Canyon windows deal with

Homes from the 1980s and 1990s often have older aluminum or wood frames with a long history of hard water behind them. Old sprinkler heads have been spraying the same panes for decades, and the spotting can run deep. On those windows, hard water removal at $10 per pane is the right step before regular cleaning. Our dual RO, 4 stage system with a DI resin tank gives near 0 TDS water, so cleaned glass stays clear. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, which covers tall canyon side walls.

Acreage means trees, dust and wildlife. Cedar pollen comes in December to February, live oak pollen in March and April, and spider webs collect around eaves and screens. Screens are $5 each, with full screens $10 and solar screens $15, and tracks are $5.

How we work Long Canyon

Acreage jobs are planned by the home, not the street. We confirm window count and gate or driveway details by text, then block time for the visit. With 200 gallons of water on board, the skid works on long drives without asking for your spigot. Large lots often have a detached garage, a workshop or a barn style outbuilding, and we can quote those too. House soft wash from $0.20 per sq ft and roof soft wash from $0.25 per sq ft pair well with windows on older stucco and stone. Most visits land within about two weeks.

A 2,300 sq ft home comes to about $230 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft, but the $200 minimum job still applies as a floor. A 6,500 sq ft home is about $650 and takes most of a day outside and inside.

Long Canyon questions

Do you serve Long Canyon off RM 2222?

Yes. Long Canyon is on our River Place route west of Loop 360. Call or text Tyler with your address and a rough square footage and we send a quote, usually within the hour during business hours.

My windows have spots that never come off. Can you fix that?

Often yes. Hard water that has baked for years is removed at $10 per pane. If the glass is already etched, we tell you what can be done before we start.

How long does an acreage home take?

Plan on about 45 to 60 minutes outside per 1,000 sq ft and about 30 minutes inside. A 4,000 sq ft home is a half day outside, and we tell you first.

Can you clean outbuildings and detached garages?

Yes. Send a window count and a photo, and we price each building. Exterior windows are $10 per window ($5 per pane) with a $200 minimum job for the visit.

Do you wash roofs and stone?

Yes. Roof soft wash starts at $0.25 per sq ft and house soft wash at $0.20 per sq ft, both with a $400 minimum. Stone and limestone are soft washed only.
