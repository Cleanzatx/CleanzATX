What Signal Hill Estates homes are like

Signal Hill Estates is one of those pockets people drive past without knowing it is there. It is a small subdivision of custom homes, priced roughly between $800K and $1.6M, with no gate and no HOA listed. Because there are not many lots, no two houses look alike. You get a mix of stone, stucco and painted siding, each built to the taste of whoever ordered it. Lots are big enough that homes sit back from each other, and plenty of owners kept the native live oaks and cedar that were already on the land. That gives the streets a settled, shaded feel even though Bee Cave retail is only minutes away.

Custom builds usually mean custom glass. Expect tall entry windows, wide sliders to the back patio, and the occasional arched or divided light unit that a standard cleaning routine does not handle well. We slow down for those.

What Signal Hill Estates windows deal with

Glass on a custom home collects more than just dust. Trees close to the house drop pollen in cedar season and again when the live oaks bloom in spring, and that yellow film bakes on once the sun hits it. Sprinkler heads aimed near the foundation leave white mineral spots on lower panes, and in this part of Travis County the water is hard enough that those spots can etch the glass if they sit for years. Dust from nearby construction along Hamilton Pool Road settles on south and west facing panes and mixes with morning dew into a gritty smear.

Screens and tracks catch the rest: leaf bits, spider webs, and fine grit that gets dragged across the glass when someone slides a door open.

How we work Signal Hill Estates

There is no gate to arrange here, so the route is simple. We park on the street or the driveway, set up the pure water system and work the outside with water fed poles that reach up to three stories from the ground. That means no ladders leaning on stucco or stone. Inside, we lay drop cloths and hand squeegee each pane. Because Signal Hill sits close to The Homestead, we often group visits with homes in both places on the same day to keep the schedule tight. Many owners add a screen cleaning or a house soft wash while we are there, since limestone and stucco get soft washed only, never blasted.

For a custom home around 3,000 square feet, exterior windows come to about $300, and we would plan roughly two and a half to three hours on site. Cleaning inside and out in Signal Hill Estates is $15 per window.

Pricing stays at $10 per window for exterior glass, or $5 per pane. Arched or divided light units take a little more time, but we do not add surprise fees. We confirm the count by text before we start.

Not for exterior glass. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we stay off the walls. Interior two story windows use ladders with cotton covers to protect your finishes.
