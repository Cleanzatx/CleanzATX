What Montevista condos are like

Montevista is a condominium community of roughly 100 units, tucked against greenbelt on the Shepherd Mountain hillside. Lake Austin is under a mile away, and trees and open green space surround the buildings. Prices run about $160K to $365K, which makes this one of the more approachable corners of the 78730 area. A condo association manages the buildings and grounds. There is no gate. Units have a smaller footprint than the single family homes nearby, usually with a balcony or small patio, a sliding door and a handful of windows.

Because the buildings stand close together, neighbors often compare notes, and a good cleaning tends to get noticed.

What Montevista windows deal with

Greenbelt is beautiful, and it also puts trees right at the glass. Pollen builds on windows and balcony doors, with cedar from December to February and live oak in March and April. Leaves and spider webs collect in screens and tracks, and dust drifts in from wooded slopes. Sprinklers on the common grounds can spray hard water on lower windows and doors, leaving white spots. Pure water from our dual RO, 4 stage system with a DI resin tank has near 0 TDS, so glass dries clear. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, which covers upper floor units.

Each screen is $5, each track is $5, and hard water spot removal is $10 per pane. Sliding door glass is priced like any other window.

How we work Montevista

For condos we keep it simple. Exterior windows are $10 per window ($5 per pane), and inside and out is $15 per window. Check the association rules on contractors and water use before booking, because some communities limit hose use or work hours; our poles use pure water and need no hookup to the common spigots. A unit with one level has an easy path to the glass. Upper floor units are done by pole from the ground, and interior is by hand with squeegees. Most condo visits are booked within a couple of weeks.

A unit of about 1,000 sq ft has roughly 10 windows, which is about $100 for the exterior. The minimum job is $200, so units with neighbors on the same day make the visit worth it. Time on site is about 45 to 60 minutes outside and about 30 inside.

Montevista Condominiums questions

Is the $200 minimum a problem for one condo?

A 1,000 sq ft unit has about 10 windows and about $100 of exterior glass, so the $200 minimum job applies. Adding interior, screens or tracks brings the total up, or a neighbor can book the same visit.

Do I need association approval?

Check your association rules on vendors. We work from the ground with poles and bring our own water, so there is usually little to approve. Tell us about any rule and we plan around it.

Can you clean balcony and sliding door glass?

Yes. Sliding doors and balcony glass count as windows, at $10 each for exterior and $15 inside and out. We cover the screens and tracks for $5 each.

Do you do upper floor units?

Yes. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so upper units do not need ladders against the building.

Can a few neighbors book together?

Yes, you can ask neighbors, and each home is its own job with its own minimum. Text us the unit count and we tell you how to schedule them on the same trip.
