What Flintrock Falls homes are like

Flintrock Falls has several hundred homes behind a gate, with the Nicklaus designed Flintrock Falls course as the centerpiece. Most of the housing is newer custom construction, in the 3,000 to 6,000 square foot range, with prices from about $1M to $3M. Membership is shared with The Hills, so the two communities feel connected even though each has its own character. Owners here tend to have large rear elevations built to look out over the fairways, with tall sliders, picture windows and glass doors.

The lots roll with the Hill Country terrain, and many homes sit on a rise with a view down a fairway. Landscaping is groomed and tends to be young enough that trees have not yet crowded the walls. Stone, stucco and metal roofs show up often. All of that glass is the reason window cleaning is a regular chore, not an occasional one.

What Flintrock Falls windows deal with

Fairway glass takes a beating from irrigation. When the sprinklers sweep toward a house, they leave a fine pattern of white mineral dots that bake on in summer sun. Left alone, those dots can etch into the glass. We use a dual RO, 4 stage pure water system with a DI resin tank, so water is near 0 TDS and the glass dries clean. Pollen is the other regular visitor, with cedar from December to February and live oak in March and April coating the sills and screens.

Newer builds also bring construction dust and stucco residue, especially in the first year. West facing glass on the back of the house shows every streak in late sun, so we finish edges and corners carefully. Screens are $5 each, tracks are $5, and hard water removal is $10 per pane for any glass that needs more than a rinse.
