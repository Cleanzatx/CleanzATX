What Bee Caves West homes are like

This is one of the older corners of Bee Cave. Homes date from the 1970s through the 1990s, and they range from about 1,200 to 3,000 square feet, with values between roughly $700K and $1.4M, driven mostly by the large lots. No gate and no HOA are listed for Bee Caves West. Many houses have been remodeled over the years, so one street can show original wood trim next to a fresh modern exterior. Lots are generous and well grown in, with oaks and cedar that were here long before the newer developments went up around them.

That age shows in the windows, which are a mix of original aluminum or wood frames and newer replacements.

What Bee Caves West windows deal with

Older frames and older glass need more care. Aluminum tracks hold decades of grit, and wooden sills can be sensitive to too much water, so we use a controlled rinse rather than flooding the frame. Mature oaks overhang many roofs, which means leaf litter and spider webs gather in screens and tracks, and heavy shade grows algae on lower panes. Sprinkler spray over the years can leave mineral scale or even start to etch glass that has never been treated. Cedar pollen from December to February and live oak pollen in spring coat everything.

Single pane units in the oldest homes show every streak, so a spot free pure water finish is a real improvement.

How we work Bee Caves West

Access is easy with no gate. We look at the frames before starting, since a loose screen or a cracked glazing bead is better found before the water goes on. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, which fits one and two story homes here without putting ladders against old siding. Indoors at Bee Caves West, panes are hand squeegeed over drop cloths. Homes with lots of tree cover often add a screen cleaning and a gutter cleaning, since oak leaves fill gutters fast. A house soft wash is gentle on older siding and brick, where blasting could do harm.

A 2,000 square foot home is about $200 for exterior windows, which is right at our $200 minimum job. Plan on about an hour and a half to two hours outside and about an hour inside.

Bee Caves West questions

Are you careful with old wood or aluminum frames?

Yes. We limit water around wood sills, clean aluminum tracks by hand and inspect frames before starting. If we see failing glazing or a loose screen, we tell you before we begin.

What is the minimum for a smaller home?

The minimum job is $200. Exterior windows are $10 each, so a home with 20 or more windows reaches it on its own, and smaller jobs are brought up to $200.

Can you handle original single pane windows?

Yes. They clean up well with pure water. If there is old mineral scale, we may suggest hard water spot removal at $10 per pane, and we explain how much it will improve.

Do you do gutters for homes with big oaks?

Yes. Gutters in Bee Caves West run $1.50, $2.25 or $3.25 per linear foot by one, two or three stories. Oak leaves and debris are bagged and removed.

Is a soft wash safe for old siding?

Yes. We use a 12V soft wash system at low pressure and a gentle cleaning mix. It is the right choice for older siding, brick and stucco. House soft wash starts at $0.20 per square foot, $400 minimum.
