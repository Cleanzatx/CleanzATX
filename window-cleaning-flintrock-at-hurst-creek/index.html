What Flintrock at Hurst Creek homes are like

This subdivision covers several hundred homes and is built around the private Flintrock Falls course. The gate keeps traffic light, and the streets curve with the land. Homes range from about 2,000 square feet up to 5,800, with prices from $700K to $2.3M, so you get a real spread of sizes. Smaller homes sit alongside large estates, and the architecture mixes Hill Country stone, stucco and some Mediterranean touches.

The Flintrock HOA looks after the common areas and the rules. Many homes have back patios with views onto the course, and plenty have pools. Because the sizes vary so much, a quote based on actual square footage makes more sense than a set price per house.

What Flintrock at Hurst Creek windows deal with

The main complaint is hard water. Sprinklers along the golf course throw mineral water onto glass and siding, and the dried spots show as a cloudy white film. Our pure water system has a dual RO, 4 stage setup and a DI resin tank. Water near 0 TDS rinses without leaving anything behind. In spring, live oak pollen turns every sill yellow, and in winter cedar pollen does the same. Creek and tree cover nearby also brings spiders, who love eaves and window corners.

Back glass that faces west picks up afternoon sun that shows streaks from any normal cleaning. That is why we finish by hand on the edges. For hardened spots, hard water removal is $10 per pane, and screens and tracks are $5 each.

How we work Flintrock at Hurst Creek

Access is simple but we plan it ahead. The subdivision is gated, so text us the address and your gate instructions, and we will check in when we arrive. We bring our own water, 200 gallons on an 8 ft skid, so there is nothing to set up inside. We often group homes in Flintrock together to keep drive time short, which is how we keep lead time around two weeks. Many owners add a driveway and patio clean, since flatwork picks up pollen and tree debris. Patios and pool decks are done by hand, so they do not get soaked.

For a typical 3,500 sq ft home here, exterior windows come to about $350. Allow about 45 to 60 minutes per 1,000 sq ft outside, so around three hours, plus about 30 minutes per 1,000 sq ft for the inside. Minimum job is $200.
