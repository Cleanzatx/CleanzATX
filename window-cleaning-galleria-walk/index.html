What Galleria Walk homes are like

Galleria Walk is made up of townhomes, with plans around 2,350 to 2,550 square feet and prices in the $700K to $1M range. There is a Galleria Walk HOA, and there is no gate. Units are tall and narrow, usually three levels, with garages on the ground floor and living space above. That layout puts a lot of glass on the front and back faces, and balconies or terraces on the upper levels. The location is the draw: restaurants and shops are a walk away, so the streets see steady foot traffic.

Because townhomes share walls, the glass is on only two sides, and each window counts.

What Galleria Walk windows deal with

Townhome glass sits close to pedestrian paths and parking, so it gets more street dust and fingerprints than glass on a private lot. Balcony doors and railings catch bird droppings and pollen. The upper floors are hard to reach with a ladder, which is why people let them go. Sprinkler systems in common landscaping are not under your control, and the overspray can leave white mineral spots on ground floor panes. Cedar pollen in winter and live oak pollen in spring coat flat surfaces like sills and balcony glass. West facing sunsets expose streaks quickly.

Because the units are tall, the third level glass is the part most owners cannot reach safely themselves.

How we work Galleria Walk

There is no gate, but parking needs thought. We park where the HOA allows, and we keep hoses and gear out of walkways. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, which fits a three level townhome exactly, with no ladder leaned against the building. Inside, we use drop cloths and hand squeegees and work quietly in shared buildings. We recommend checking with your HOA about any posted rules for contractors. Townhomes are a good match for the Twice a Year plan at 10% off, and neighbors can book the same day to make a visit efficient.

A 2,450 square foot unit is about $245 for exterior windows, which is above the $200 minimum. Plan on about two hours outside and roughly an hour and a quarter inside.

Galleria Walk questions

Can you reach the third floor windows?

Yes. Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so all levels on a typical townhome are covered. Interior glass is done by hand.

Pure water leaves no soap, and we control the rinse. We keep runoff off walkways as much as possible, and we cover nothing that does not need covering.
