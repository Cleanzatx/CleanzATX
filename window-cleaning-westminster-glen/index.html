What Westminster Glen homes are like

Lots in Westminster Glen run from 1 to 4.6 acres, which is a lot of room for northwest Austin. The custom houses were built between 1986 and 2009, so the neighborhood spans several decades of design. Prices run from about $2 million to $3 million. The Westminster Glen HOA oversees the community, and there is no gate.

On acreage, the house usually sits far back from the road, with oaks and cedars in the front and views or hillside behind. Each home is its own layout, so no two jobs look alike.

What Westminster Glen windows deal with

Custom homes usually mean more glass, taller glass and glass in odd places: transoms, arched tops, clerestory windows and large picture panes. Older houses from the 1980s and 1990s may have divided lights with many small panes, and those take time because every pane has its own edge.

Irrigation across big lawns leaves hard water marks on any glass it reaches, and years of marks can etch. Heavy tree cover adds pollen, webs and leaf litter. Cedar pollen peaks in winter, and live oak pollen in spring.

How we work Westminster Glen

For custom homes we start with photos by text, then plan the visit for the number of windows and levels. Poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so two story foyers and high gables are within reach. Interior two story glass is done from covered ladders over drop cloths. Houses of this age usually have a roof that has weathered, and a soft wash removes black streaks without harsh pressure. Tyler will advise what needs attention first.

As a sample, a 5,000 square foot home is about $500 for exterior glass and takes around five hours. Divided light windows may add time, so he quotes those by pane.

Westminster Glen questions

How do you price a custom home with many small panes?

Exterior windows are $10 per window or $5 per pane. Divided light windows with lots of panes are counted by pane, and we confirm the total from your photos before the visit.

Can you reach tall entry and stair windows?

Exterior windows up to three stories are cleaned from the ground with water fed poles. Interior two story glass is cleaned from ladders with cotton covers and drop cloths over your floors.

Is my older home's glass at risk of etching?

If hard water has sat for years, it can etch. Pure water cleaning prevents new damage. For existing marks we offer hard water spot removal at $10 per pane.

Do you wash roofs on acreage homes?

Yes. A 12V soft wash system handles roofs from $0.25 per square foot with a $400 minimum. We never use high pressure on a roof, and limestone is only ever soft washed.

Will the HOA need to approve you?

There is no gate to pass. If the Westminster Glen HOA has vendor notice rules, tell us in your text and we follow them.
