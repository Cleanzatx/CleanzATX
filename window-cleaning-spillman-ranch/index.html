What Spillman Ranch homes are like

Spillman Ranch Loop curves along the edge of the Falconhead course, and the homes that line it are priced from roughly $600,000 to $1.1 million. It is a small section of Falconhead, so the Falconhead HOA applies and the streets are open. Owners here chose the location for the green space, and many back walls look straight onto turf and mature trees. Walls are a mix of stucco and Hill Country limestone, with covered back patios that frame the golf views.

Window sets follow that idea: a wide back wall of glass, smaller panes on the street side, and a few tall entry windows up front.

Spillman Ranch Loop

What Spillman Ranch windows deal with

Next to a course, the water that waters the grass also waters your glass. Overspray from irrigation leaves hard water spots, usually in a fan pattern across the lowest panes. They are easy to ignore for a season and hard to remove after three. We treat them at $10 per pane, and our filtered rinse keeps new spots from forming while we work.

Turf also brings fine dust and clippings, and the open sightline means wind carries pollen onto the back wall in spring. Cedar pollen from December to February arrives first, followed by live oak. Stucco and limestone on a golf side wall collect a green cast in the shade, and the 12V soft wash lifts it without scarring the stone.

Spillman Ranch questions

What causes the fan shaped spots on my glass?

That pattern comes from sprinkler overspray. Hard water in the irrigation dries into mineral spots. They can be removed, and the longer they sit the more they bond to the glass.

Should I move my patio furniture?

It helps. We keep water off cushions and fabrics where possible, but clearing the area near the back wall makes setup quicker and keeps everything dry.

Do you clean outdoor living areas as well?

Yes. Patios and pool decks are cleaned by hand so they do not get soaked, and flatwork like driveways uses a surface cleaner at $0.18 per square foot for concrete.

Are you familiar with Falconhead HOA rules?

We work throughout Falconhead and keep to normal daytime hours. If your HOA has a specific request, tell us when you text and we follow it.

What is the price for a typical home?

Exterior windows run $10 each or about $0.10 per square foot of home, so a 3,000 square foot house is near $300. The minimum job is $200.
