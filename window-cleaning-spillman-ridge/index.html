What Spillman Ridge homes are like

Spillman Ridge runs along high ground off Spillman Ranch Loop, one of the smaller sections inside Falconhead. Homes are priced from about $600,000 to $1 million, which puts them at the approachable end of the community. The Falconhead HOA covers it, and there is no gate. Because it sits on a ridge, many houses have views across Bee Cave and the Hill Country, and with views come large panes meant to show them.

Exteriors lean toward stucco with limestone accents. That combination looks sharp when clean and picks up dust fast, because the textured surface holds on to it.

Streets we work: Spillman Ranch Loop.

What Spillman Ridge windows deal with

View glass is the biggest chore. Large fixed panes at the back of a ridge house take wind, dust and pollen straight on, and afternoon sun makes every smear obvious. Where sprinklers hit the lower panes, hard water leaves white spots that need a dedicated removal step, which is $10 per pane. Cedar season runs December to February, then live oak from March through April.

Our pure water system uses dual RO and a DI resin tank, bringing the water to near 0 TDS. It rinses clean and dries without a film, which is what keeps big panes from streaking. Stucco and limestone are soft washed with the 12V system so the stone is never blasted.

How we work Spillman Ridge

This is an open street layout, so we park, set up poles and get going with no access steps. We usually book Spillman Ridge in the same trip as the nearby Spillman Ranch homes and the rest of Falconhead. For homes with a lot of back glass, we start there while the sun is low, and finish with the front. A house soft wash is a natural add on here, because stucco and limestone show dust more than a flat painted wall would.

As a plain example, a 2,500 square foot home is about $250 for exterior windows and takes roughly one and three quarter to two and a half hours. Inside and out would be about $375 at $15 a window.

Spillman Ridge questions

Do you clean large picture windows?

Yes, and they are our favorite kind. Pure water dries with no spots and no soap film, so large panes stay even and clear.

Can you reach second story glass without ladders?

Our water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground. That covers most exterior glass on ridge homes without a ladder on the house.

What does it cost to wash my stucco?

Stucco soft washing is priced from $0.20 per square foot, $400 minimum, using a 12V system that is gentle on stone.

Will you remove the white spots on my glass?

Hard water spots can usually be removed for $10 per pane. We check how long they have been there first, since old etching may not come out completely.

How soon can you come?

Most Spillman Ridge visits land within two weeks of the quote. We never promise a date up front, and the quote itself comes by text.
