What Vista Ridge Estates is like

Think of Vista Ridge Estates as the larger sibling of Vista Ridge. It came from the same 2018 to 2019 phase, but the lots are estate size and the homes start at $1.5M and go up. It is not gated, and the Rough Hollow HOA covers it.

With bigger houses come longer glass lines and more of them. Expect big picture windows, tall entry glass, and covered outdoor rooms with sliders. Because the homes are fairly new, they are mostly in good condition with a few years of buildup. Because estate homes sit back from the street, it helps if you let us know where the best place to stage the truck is.

What Vista Ridge Estates windows deal with

The estate lots have large lawns, and sprinkler heads near the house send hard water onto the lower glass. White rings form quickly and etch if left for months. The tall panes are the second issue, since a glass wall catches afternoon light and reveals every smear.

Ridge breezes bring dust too. Tracks fill with it, and live oak pollen from March and April ties it into a gritty paste. We clean tracks at $5 each and screens at $5, full screens $10, solar screens $15.

How we work Vista Ridge Estates

Estate jobs get a full morning, sometimes a full day for the bigger homes. We bring pure water on the truck, so we do not need your outdoor tap, and the poles keep us off roofs and ladders. This section is a natural pair with Vista Ridge for scheduling, and we often finish the neighboring home the same day.

A 5,500 sq ft home is about $550 for exterior windows, taking five to six hours outside. A full inside and out clean adds time at $15 a window. A Every 2 Months plan takes 20% off for owners who want it spotless year round.

Vista Ridge Estates questions

Do estate homes cost more to clean?

The price follows window count or square footage. At about $0.10 per sq ft, a larger home costs more, but the rate does not change.

Do you need access to my outside tap?

No. The truck carries pure water, which means we do not use your hose bib for the glass.

Can you handle the glass around covered patios?

Yes. We clean sliders, transoms and tall panes. The patio itself can be cleaned by hand, so water is kept off furniture.

What if I am not home?

That is fine for exterior work as long as gates are unlocked and pets are inside. For interior work we need someone to let us in.

How do you price a very large home?

We use about $0.10 per sq ft or $10 per window, whichever is a better fit for the house. A 6,000 sq ft home is about $600 for the outside. Text a photo or the square footage and we confirm.

Can the visit be split over two days?

Sometimes. On the biggest homes we may suggest doing the outside first and the interior on a second visit, so neither day runs long. We do not promise specific dates, but we can talk it through when you text.
