What Cardinal Hills homes are like

This subdivision sits off RM 620, close to Hudson Bend and the shore of Lake Travis, with a Lakeway extraterritorial jurisdiction address and Austin mail. Homes range from small 1,000 square foot houses up to 4,600 square foot custom builds. Prices start around the mid $400Ks and reach $1.3M. Because it was built over three decades, one street can include a 1970s ranch, a 1990s two story and a new rebuild. A voluntary neighborhood association exists, and there is no gate.

The neighborhood has a settled feel. Trees have had time to grow, yards are established and many owners have lived here for a long time. People tend to care about upkeep, and clean glass is the easiest improvement to see from the curb.

What Cardinal Hills windows deal with

Big trees are the main factor. Oaks and cedar shade the glass and drop leaves and seeds into screens and tracks. Spiders favor shady frames, so webs return fast. Pollen is heavy in season: cedar from December to February, live oak in March and April.

Older windows often have wood or aluminum frames and tracks with years of grime built up. Newer vinyl replacements are easier but still collect pollen film. If you live closer to the water, spray and sprinkler water leave white spots that we treat at $10 per pane. Pure water then dries the glass clean and leaves no soap film behind.

How we work Cardinal Hills

There is no gate to plan around. We park at the curb or in the driveway and use poles to reach upper glass from the ground, so ladders stay off older siding and trim. Screens come off, get brushed and go back, and tracks get cleaned by hand at $5 each. Houses on bigger lots often want the roof soft washed or the driveway cleaned, so we price those as one visit. Because Cardinal Hills is close to Lakeway, scheduling usually fits into a normal two week window.

A 2,500 square foot home is about $250 for exterior windows at $10 per window. Plan on about two to two and a half hours outside, and about 75 minutes more for the inside. Smaller homes still meet the $200 minimum.
