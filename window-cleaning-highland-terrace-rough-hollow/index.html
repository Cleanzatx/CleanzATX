What Highland Terrace is like

Highland Terrace came in the 2018 to 2019 phase and sits near Highland Village, the amenity hub for Rough Hollow. Homes are production built, with more consistent floor plans than the custom hilltop sections. Prices run around $700K to $1.2M. It is not gated.

Repeating floor plans help us. We know roughly how many windows to expect and where the tall ones are, so quotes are fast and accurate. Many homes have an upper floor with a bank of windows over the front door and a rear living area with large glass. The location also makes the section easy to reach, and visits stay short.

What Highland Terrace windows deal with

A short walk to the amenity center means active households and plenty of foot traffic, so front glass gets handprints and fingerprints. Rear glass collects dust and pollen from the ridge. Builders also leave residue on newer glass.

Sprinklers matter too. Production lots generally have modest yards, so the heads often sit close to the house and spray the lower panes. Hard water removal is $10 per pane. Screens and tracks are $5 each, and we usually recommend doing them together.

How we work Highland Terrace

Because the plans repeat, we move quickly and the visit is easy to predict. Highland Terrace is close to Canyon Pass and Arroyo Divide, so one morning can cover several homes. Parking is straightforward and the HOA allows normal service work.

A 3,000 sq ft home is about $300 for exterior windows, taking around two to two and a half hours outside. Inside adds about 90 minutes. With a Twice a Year plan you save 10%, and members on higher plans also receive a free yearly screen clean.

Highland Terrace questions

Are all Highland Terrace plans the same?

Many share similar plans, but each home has its own window count and upgrades. We quote by window count or square footage, and a text with your address is enough to start.

Do you clean screens in the same visit?

Yes. Standard screens cost $5, full ones $10 and solar ones $15. Each one comes down, gets cleaned, and goes back up.

How soon can you start?

Booking usually lands within two weeks. We do not offer same day service, but you will get a clear arrival window.

Do you offer plans for smaller yards?

Yes. All Clean Windows Club plans work the same regardless of lot size. Twice a Year saves 10%, Triannual 15%, Quarterly 15% and Every 2 Months 20%.

Do you clean the windows inside the garage and side doors?

Yes. Garage door lights, side door glass and any small windows around the house are counted at the same $10 each. Just point them out when you text.

Can I split exterior and interior over two visits?

Yes. Exterior is $10 a window and interior can be added later, though doing both at once at $15 a window is simpler and a little more efficient.
