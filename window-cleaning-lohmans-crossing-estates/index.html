What Lohmans Crossing Estates homes are like

The subdivision is built in Sections 1 through 3 and holds several hundred homes. Plans are newer and energy efficient, with open layouts that bring in a lot of light. Sizes cover a wide range, from about 1,400 square feet to 5,600, and prices run between $500K and $1.1M. The Lohmans Crossing Estates HOA oversees the community, and there is no gate.

Because homes range so widely in size, one street can include a modest single story and a large two story. That makes this a place where a flat price per home does not fit, and we price by the square foot or by the window.

What Lohmans Crossing Estates windows deal with

Energy efficient glass often has a low emissivity coating, and that coating shows streaks and hard water spots clearly. Soap film left behind by a hurried cleaning is easy to see in morning and evening light. Pure water, with near 0 TDS, leaves glass without that film.

The area is still growing, so construction dust drifts across lots and settles on windows and screens. Cedar pollen arrives from December to February, live oak pollen in March and April. Sprinkler overspray leaves spots on lower panes. For spots already baked on, removal is $10 per pane.

Lohmans Crossing Estates questions

Is energy efficient glass cleaned differently?

It is cleaned the same way, with pure water and soft tools. We avoid abrasive pads and scrapers that can scratch coatings. Streaks show more clearly on coated glass, so a spot free finish matters.

Why is there dust on my windows every week?

New construction nearby sends fine dust through the air. Plans such as Every 2 Months or Quarterly keep ahead of it with 15% to 20% off.

How are small and large plans priced?

About $0.10 per square foot for exterior windows, or $10 per window. The minimum job is $200, which covers the smaller 1,400 square foot plans.

Can you reach the tall stair windows?

Yes. From the ground, our water fed poles reach as high as three stories. Inside, we use cotton covered ladders and drop cloths for stair glass.

What if the spots come back?

Send a photo within 7 days. We return free for a touch up when we are in the area, and plan customers also get the 7 day rain guarantee.
