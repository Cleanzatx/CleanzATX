The Cleanz ATX Difference

We don't show up with a bucket. We show up with a rig.

Every truck rolls up stocked and organized before we knock on your door: a pure-water filtration system, water-fed poles, a Honda-powered pressure washer, and ladders racked and ready. Nothing is improvised in your driveway, and nothing changes from one visit to the next.

  • Pure water that dries spot-free

    Our RO/DI system strips the minerals out of Lake Travis hard water before it touches your glass, so windows dry clear with no soap film and no spots.

  • Reach up to three stories from the ground

    Water-fed poles clean second- and third-story glass from your yard, so there's no ladder leaning on your stucco or stone for most windows.

  • The same setup on every job

    Organized, commercial-grade gear means we run every home the same way, so the clean you get in spring matches the one you get in fall.

FAQ

Window Cleaning Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before booking.

Exterior window cleaning is $10 per window (or $5 per pane). Interior cleaning adds $5 per window, and screens are normally $5 each. The minimum job is $150 and a typical home runs about $350. Request a free quote and we'll text you your exact price.

We run tap water through reverse osmosis and de-ionization (RO/DI) filters that strip out the dissolved minerals, then rinse your glass with water-fed poles. With the minerals gone, there's nothing left to dry into spots or streaks, and no soap residue. Spots only show up when a pure-water system isn't maintained and minerals get through, so we service our filters and check our water quality to keep it pure.

We use water-fed poles that reach windows up to three stories from the ground, so there's no ladder leaning on your stucco or stone for most windows. Ladders are used only where a pole can't reach.

Usually within the hour during business hours. We text your price and our next open dates from (512) 518-0512, and you can book by replying.

Exterior windows on a typical 3,000 sq ft home take about 1.5–2 hours. Inside-and-out cleanings and larger homes take longer; we'll tell you the expected time with your quote.

Not for exterior-only cleaning, as long as we can get into the yard. For interior cleaning, someone needs to be home. We confirm the details before your appointment.

If rain affects your results within 7 days of your service, we come back and re-clean for free. We also offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee: if anything is missed or streaked, we fix it at no charge.
