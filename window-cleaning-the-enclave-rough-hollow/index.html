What The Enclave is like

The Enclave is described as the only interior section of Rough Hollow that is gated. It is a small section, with homes starting at about $1M. It sits inside Rough Hollow, away from the shoreline, so views are more of the neighborhood and hills than the lake.

The gate gives it a private feel, with less drive by traffic and quieter streets. The Rough Hollow HOA applies here. Homes here are mostly newer, and the stucco and stone styling matches the rest of the community.

What Enclave windows deal with

Being inland, the glass sees less lake spray, though sprinkler water is still hard and leaves spots. Quiet streets mean less road dust, but pollen still lands on sills and screens during cedar season and oak pollen season.

Homes in this price range often have large picture windows and tall entries, which show every smear. We use pure water, so there is no film, and poles keep ladders off the walls. Rooms with high ceilings often have a second row of windows high on the wall, and those are the ones most often missed on a do it yourself clean.

How we work The Enclave

Gate access is arranged first. You tell us how to get through, whether by code, a name on the list or meeting us, and we follow it. We schedule The Enclave next to other interior sections for routing. We text when we are on the way, and again when the work is done.

A 4,000 sq ft Enclave home is about $400 for exterior windows, taking about three hours outside. Inside adds about two hours. Plan members on Quarterly get 15% off and the 7 day rain guarantee.

The Enclave questions

How do you get through the gate?

You tell us what works, such as a code or a name on the list, and we follow it. We never go in without your say so.

Is The Enclave really gated?

The research we have says it is the only interior section described as gated. Peninsula, on the water, has its own guard gate.

Do you clean the whole house exterior?

Yes. Soft wash on stucco starts at $0.20 per sq ft, with a $400 minimum.

Do you clean gutters?

Yes, at $1.50, $2.25 or $3.25 a foot depending on stories.

What if the gate code changes?

Just text us the new code or let us know the new plan. Because the gate is yours to manage, we only go in the way you tell us.

Do you also soft wash roofs here?

Yes. Roof soft wash starts at $0.25 per sq ft with a $400 minimum, and it uses the 12V system, with no high pressure on the shingles or tile.
