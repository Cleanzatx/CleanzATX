What Hudson Bend homes are like

Hudson Bend is one of the most mixed areas on the lake. Roughly 1,000 homes sit on the peninsula, and no two streets feel alike. You get small older cabins that were built as weekend places, ranch houses that have been added onto over the years, and newer waterfront estates priced up to $2.5M. Homes start around $435K. Most of the area has no HOA, so owners make their own calls about upkeep, and a lot of them do it one project at a time.

The peninsula is made up of several older plats, including Hudson Bend Colony, Travis Landing, Eck Lane and Brill Arno. Marinas sit along the shore, and the main basin of the lake wraps around three sides of the land. That means the water is close to almost every address, even when the house itself is a few lots back.

Streets we work: Hudson Bend Road, Pool Canyon, Eck Lane, Brill Arno, Travis Landing.

What Hudson Bend windows deal with

With this much shoreline, lake spray and wind blown mist reach glass that sits well back from the water. Lake water is hard, and when it dries on a window it leaves white spots. Left for a few seasons those spots can etch into the glass and stop coming off with a normal wash. We treat hard water spots at $10 per pane, and the earlier we get to them the better the result.

Older cabins often have single story rooflines and low windows that collect dust and pollen from the road. Waterfront estates have tall glass facing the lake, and that glass shows every streak when the afternoon sun swings around. Cedar pollen in winter and live oak pollen in spring land on all of it.

How we work Hudson Bend

With no gates and mostly no HOA, scheduling here is simple. We park, run the pure water system from the truck, and reach exterior glass up to three stories with water fed poles, so no ladders go against your house. The peninsula is a long drive in and out, so we like to group jobs by plat or road. If a neighbor books the same week, we can often line the visits up back to back. Pairing window cleaning with a house soft wash makes sense on older siding or limestone.

A 2,500 sq ft home here comes to about $250 for exterior windows at $0.10 per sq ft, and the minimum job is $200. Plan on roughly two to two and a half hours on site for the outside of a home that size.

Hudson Bend questions

Does lake spray really damage windows on Hudson Bend?

It can. Lake water is hard, and mist that dries on glass leaves white mineral spots. Over time those spots can etch the surface. Cleaning with pure water on a regular schedule keeps minerals from building up, and we remove existing spots at $10 per pane.

Do you clean older cabins and smaller homes on the peninsula?

Yes. Pricing is $10 per window or about $0.10 per sq ft, with a $200 minimum job. A small cabin may fall under the minimum, so we will tell you that up front when we quote by text.

Do you work on homes without an HOA?

Yes, and most of Hudson Bend has none. That keeps it easy. You text us, we send a quote, and we pick a day. Typical lead time is within two weeks, though we do not promise a specific date.

Can you clean glass facing the lake on a tall waterfront house?

Water fed poles reach up to three stories from the ground, so we can do most exterior glass without putting ladders on your house. Interior glass is done by hand, with drop cloths and cotton covered ladders for two story rooms.

What if spots show up after the wash?

Text a photo within 7 days. If we are in the area, we come back and touch it up free. Every job also carries our 100% satisfaction guarantee, and Tyler handles that conversation himself.

Do you also wash the siding and stone here?

Yes. House soft wash starts at $0.20 per sq ft with a $400 minimum. We soft wash limestone and stucco and never pressure wash limestone, since that can scar it.
