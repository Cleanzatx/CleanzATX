Window Cleaning Conditions in Hudson Bend

Hudson Bend is the peninsula on the south shore of Lake Travis between Lakeway and Mansfield Dam, reached from RM 620 by Hudson Bend Road. Much of it is lakefront or lake-view property: a mix of full-time homes, weekend places and vacation rentals, most of them built to face the water.

Lake-side windows take the brunt of the weather. Wind off the open water carries dust and pollen, and homes close to the shoreline also catch lake spray. Both Lake Travis water and local tap water are hard, so whatever dries on the glass leaves white mineral spots that build up over time and get harder to remove.

Because many Hudson Bend homes sit empty between visits, that buildup can go unnoticed for months. A quarterly or twice-a-year plan keeps lake-facing windows clear whether you're in town or not, and we can power wash patios and dock areas on the same visit.
